# q8 — Computational / mathematical / neuroscientific formalisation of psychoanalytic (esp. Lacanian) concepts: state of the art and rigor

Research date: 2026-09-11. Budget used: 8 web searches, 6 fetches (2 fetches failed: wikidot HTTP-only redirect loop; HAL page blocked by Anubis). Everything marked VERIFIED was seen on a publisher/indexer page or in a fetched full text during this session; UNVERIFIED items are from background knowledge and must be checked before citation (volume/page/DOI in particular).

## Findings

### 1. Matte Blanco's bi-logic and its formal treatments

- Ignacio Matte Blanco, *The Unconscious as Infinite Sets: An Essay in Bi-logic* (Duckworth 1975; Karnac reissue 1998, ISBN 9780715607183) — VERIFIED (publisher/retail pages). Core claims: the unconscious obeys a "symmetric logic" (any relation is treated as symmetric; a part is treated as identical to the whole, hence "infinite sets" in the Dedekind sense), stratified against ordinary "asymmetric" (Aristotelian) logic; the five Freudian characteristics of the system Ucs (timelessness, absence of contradiction, displacement, condensation, replacement of external by internal reality) are derived from the symmetry principle. Matte Blanco's own mathematics is informal: he uses set-theoretic vocabulary (Cantor/Dedekind infinite sets, equivalence classes) as an analogy, not as an axiomatised system. The most sympathetic formal reader on record (Joncas 2021, see below) says outright: "His use of mathematics is somewhat shallow, leaving many open questions."
- Formal treatments that exist (each is a *reconstruction*, none is a canonical formalisation accepted by clinicians):
  - **Rayner (1995)**, *Unconscious Logic: An Introduction to Matte Blanco's Bi-Logic and Its Uses*, Routledge — chaos-theory framing; assessed by Joncas as "undeveloped analogies". VERIFIED (secondary, via Joncas).
  - **Lauro-Grotto (2008)**, "The Unconscious as an Ultrametric Set", *American Imago* 64(4) — reads symmetric logic as an ultrametric (tree/hierarchical-clustering) space, in which every triangle is isosceles and "distance" collapses inside clusters. VERIFIED (secondary, via Joncas). This is the one treatment with a credible dual mathematical/clinical author.
  - **Murtagh (2012, 2014, ...)**, "Ultrametric model of mind, I & II", *p-Adic Numbers, Ultrametric Analysis and Applications* 4(3); later text-analytic experiments (hierarchical clustering of narrative/dream text as empirical proxy for "condensation"). VERIFIED (secondary, via Joncas). This is the only Matte Blanco line with an empirical pipeline.
  - **Khrennikov** — p-adic / m-adic tree models of "mental space" with conscious/unconscious dynamics (e.g., Khrennikov 2007, *Biosystems* 90(3)). UNVERIFIED (from memory). Mathematically rigorous as dynamics on p-adic trees, but the psychological mapping is by fiat.
  - **Battilotti** — sequent-calculus and quantum-logic readings of bi-logic: "A Predicative Characterization of Quantum States and Matte Blanco's Bi-logic" (Springer LNCS, *Quantum Interaction*, doi 10.1007/978-3-642-54943-4_16) — VERIFIED (Springer page); "Interpreting Matte Blanco's Bi-logic by sequents" — VERIFIED (academia.edu record); "Symmetry vs. Duality in Logic: An Interpretation of Bi-Logic to Model Cognitive Processes Beyond Inference", *Int. J. Cognitive Informatics and Natural Intelligence* (ISSN 1557-3958) — VERIFIED (Gale record). Battilotti, Borozan & Lauro Grotto (2023), "The Modal Components of Judgements in a Quantum Model of Psychoanalytic Theory", *Entropy* 25(7):1057, PubMed 37510004 — VERIFIED (title/venue); author list UNVERIFIED. Technically the most rigorous work in this sub-field (proof-theoretic, with "virtual singletons" implementing the symmetric mode), but the quantum machinery is contested: Joncas's objection — "quantum logic brings in way too much extra baggage, such as spin operators. This can't be justified unless we literally believe mental objects follow quantum rules."
  - **Iurato (2018)**, *Computational Psychoanalysis and Formal Bi-Logic Frameworks*, IGI Global, ISBN 9781522541288 — VERIFIED (IGI Global, PhilPapers). This book is the source of the phrase **"computational psychoanalysis"** as a book title. It applies group-theoretic symmetry-breaking to bi-logic and also to Lacanian mathemes. Joncas's verdict: "there is no actual good-faith engagement, such as through sustained examples, but only tossing around undeveloped analogies."
  - **Tomic (2020)**, "Quantum Computational Psychoanalysis: Quantum Logic Approach to Bi-logic", arXiv working paper — VERIFIED (secondary, via Joncas).
  - **Joncas (2021)**, "Computational Psychoanalysis" (blog essay, gjoncas.github.io, 2021-02-16) — VERIFIED (fetched). Useful as the only critical survey of this literature; it is a blog, not peer-reviewed.
- Rigor verdict: the ultrametric line (Lauro-Grotto/Murtagh) is checkable (hierarchical clustering is a well-defined algorithm and ultrametricity is a testable property of a distance matrix); Battilotti's sequent calculi are checkable as logic; the group-theoretic and chaos-theoretic lines are analogical. No treatment has clinical validation beyond illustrative examples.

### 2. Neuropsychoanalysis and active inference (Solms, Friston, Carhart-Harris, Hopkins)

- **Carhart-Harris & Friston (2010)**, "The default-mode, ego-functions and free-energy: a neurobiological account of Freudian ideas", *Brain* 133(4):1265–1283 — VERIFIED (OUP page: academic.oup.com/brain/article/133/4/1265/307446); DOI 10.1093/brain/awq010 UNVERIFIED (from memory). Maps Freud's primary/secondary process onto free-energy minimisation in a hierarchical generative model: the "ego" = the default-mode / higher-level cortical hierarchy that suppresses (explains away) prediction error from limbic/primary-process activity; "primary process" = unconstrained, high-entropy activity; "secondary process" = precision-weighted top-down constraint. Level of formality: the free-energy equations are Friston's standard ones; the Freudian mapping is verbal (an interpretive identification of terms, not derived predictions). It is the canonical citation for "Freud ↔ FEP".
- **Carhart-Harris & Friston (2019)**, "REBUS and the Anarchic Brain: Toward a Unified Model of the Brain Action of Psychedelics", *Pharmacological Reviews* 71(3):316–344, DOI 10.1124/pr.118.017160 — UNVERIFIED (from memory; not fetched). Extends the 2010 paper: psychedelics "RElax Beliefs Under pSychedelics" by lowering the precision of high-level priors, releasing bottom-up (primary-process) signal. Same formal status: the mechanism is stated in FEP vocabulary, with the Freudian reading as interpretation; some predictions (entropy of spontaneous activity, DMN disintegration) are empirical and have been tested in imaging.
- **Solms (2019)**, "The hard problem of consciousness and the free energy principle", *Frontiers in Psychology* 9:2714, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02714 — VERIFIED (fetched). Consciousness = affect = "felt uncertainty"; brainstem homeostatic regulation; precision (ω) is identified with consciousness itself. Verbatim: "deviation away from a homeostatic settling point (increasing uncertainty) is felt as unpleasure, and returning toward it (decreasing uncertainty) is felt as pleasure." Formal content: one free-energy/prediction-error equation with precision weighting; the rest is conceptual mapping of anatomy, phylogeny, and dual-aspect monism onto it. Solms concedes the "conceivability" objection (the function could be done unconsciously) and that tests are indirect (lesion/pharmacology/consolidation predictions).
- **Solms & Friston (2018)**, "How and why consciousness arises: some considerations from physics and physiology", *Journal of Consciousness Studies* 25(5–6):202–238 — UNVERIFIED. The joint paper underlying the above.
- **Solms (2021)**, *The Hidden Spring: A Journey to the Source of Consciousness*, Profile Books / W.W. Norton — UNVERIFIED (not fetched; well-known). Popular-book form of the same thesis, including the claim that an "artificial consciousness" built on these principles is possible in principle.
- **Hopkins (2016)**, "Free Energy and Virtual Reality in Neuroscience and Psychoanalysis: A Complexity Theory of Dreaming and Mental Disorder", *Frontiers in Psychology* 7:922 — UNVERIFIED (from memory). Jim Hopkins (philosopher) uses FEP to re-derive Freudian dream-as-wish-fulfilment and the role of conflict/complexity; also Hopkins in *Consciousness and the Free Energy Principle* discussions. Formal status: conceptual.
- Other FEP–psychoanalysis papers worth knowing (all UNVERIFIED as to volume/DOI): Connolly (2018), "Expected Free Energy Formalizes Conflict Underlying Defense in Freudian Psychoanalysis", *Front. Psychol.* 9:1264 (the one paper that actually writes down an expected-free-energy expression for a *defence*); Holmes & Nolte (2019), "'Surprise' and the Bayesian Brain: Implications for Psychotherapy Theory and Practice", *Front. Psychol.* 10:592; Rabeyron & Massicotte (2020), "Entropy, Free Energy, and Symbolization: Free Association at the Intersection of Psychoanalysis and Neuroscience", *Front. Psychol.* 11:366; Cieri & Esposito (2019), *Front. Psychol.* 10:1983.
- Rigor verdict: the FEP side is mathematically rigorous (variational inference); the psychoanalytic side is a *dictionary* (id ↔ high-entropy lower levels, ego ↔ precision-weighted priors, repression ↔ explaining-away, pleasure ↔ reduction of expected free energy). None of these papers models a Lacanian construct (signifier, objet a, four discourses, jouissance); they are Freudian. The corpus's use of "free energy" to name a Kramers/cusp potential is *not* the variational free energy of the FEP, and the treatises should say so explicitly to avoid an equivocation that reviewers will catch.

### 3. Lacan and cybernetics / information theory

- **Lacan, Seminar II (1954–55)**, *The Ego in Freud's Theory and in the Technique of Psychoanalysis* (Norton 1988), lecture "Psychoanalysis and Cybernetics, or On the Nature of Language" (22 June 1955), plus the "Introduction" and "Parenthesis of Parentheses" appended to the *Écrits* "Seminar on 'The Purloined Letter'" (Écrits 1966; Fink translation 2006). The even/odd guessing game (from Poe) is used to show that a "purely symbolic" sequence of +/− coin tosses, once recoded into overlapping triples (1, 2, 3) and then into the α β γ δ alphabet, has *forbidden successions* — i.e., memory and "law" emerge from the syntax of the chain alone. UNVERIFIED as to page numbers, but the content is textbook. Important for the treatises: this is the one place where Lacan's own text specifies a genuinely checkable finite-state constraint (a 4-symbol language with excluded transitions); it is the legitimate ancestor of any "signifying chain as automaton / Markov chain" model. Bruce Fink's chapter "The Nature of Unconscious Thought, or Why No One Ever Reads Lacan's Postface to the 'Seminar on The Purloined Letter'" (in Feldstein, Fink & Jaanus eds., *Reading Seminars I and II*, SUNY 1996) works through the automaton — UNVERIFIED (from memory).
- **Lydia H. Liu (2010)**, "The Cybernetic Unconscious: Rethinking Lacan, Poe, and French Theory", *Critical Inquiry* 36(2):288–320 — UNVERIFIED as to volume/pages (from memory). **Liu (2010)**, *The Freudian Robot: Digital Media and the Future of the Unconscious*, University of Chicago Press, ISBN 9780226486840; chapter 4 "The Cybernetic Unconscious" (doi 10.7208/9780226486840-007) — VERIFIED (UCP, bibliovault, De Gruyter chapter page). Liu documents that Lacan's 1955 game-theoretic/Markov treatment of the chain was a direct response to Shannon, Wiener, von Neumann–Morgenstern and the Macy conferences (via Guilbaud and Riguet), and that the "symbolic order" is in part a rewrite of information theory's notion of a code. Historical, not formal, but it is the scholarly warrant for saying "Lacan's chain is an information-theoretic object".
- **John Johnston (2008)**, *The Allure of Machinic Life: Cybernetics, Artificial Life, and the New AI*, MIT Press — VERIFIED existence (retail/library pages); its Lacan/cybernetics chapter content UNVERIFIED. (If the intended reference is Adrian Johnston, no "Lacan and cybernetics" title by him was found; the Guido/Mieli "Lacan and Cybernetics" seminar PDF, 2024, is a lecture handout — VERIFIED existence, unrefereed.)
- Rigor verdict: the historical claim (Lacan drew on Shannon/Wiener) is well established; the only *formal* object is the α β γ δ automaton in Seminar II / Écrits, which is small and fully checkable.

### 4. Direct computational models of Lacanian constructs

- **Gadalla, Nikoletseas & de A. Amazonas (2026)**, "Combining psychoanalytic concepts and computer science methodologies: an empirical study of the relationship between emotions and the Lacanian discourses", *Frontiers in Psychology*, section Quantitative Psychology and Measurement, published 23 March 2026, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1526215 — VERIFIED (fetched full text). Affiliations: University of Patras; CTI "Diophantus"; Technical University of Catalonia.
  - Method ("Lacanian Discourse Discovery", LDD): 40 dialogues (264 sentences) sampled from the DailyDialog corpus; 32 discrete emotions (30 from Google's GoEmotions taxonomy + "anguish" and "anxiety" from the psychoanalytic literature); three raters annotate each sentence with up to three emotions (with confidence scores) and with discourse labels (with confidence scores and a 0–1 weight) for **five** discourses — Master, University, Analyst, Hysteric, **Capitalist**. They define a conditional probability P(d₁…dₙ | e₁…eₗ) as a normalised sum of products of rater confidence scores (their Eq. 4), a "weight level" (Def. 3.1, product of discourse weights over co-occurring sentences, summed), and a "relation intensity" in [0,1] (Def. 3.3) combining the two; Theorem 3.1 claims the estimator is unbiased (expectation argument). Output: emotion × discourse heat-maps of relation intensity.
  - Validation: inter-rater reliability only — Krippendorff's α across three raters: Analyst 0.70, University 0.60, Master/Capitalist 0.55, Hysteric 0.52; best pair 0.71–0.80. No held-out prediction, no classifier, no external criterion. The paper frames itself as groundwork for automating discourse identification with AI, not as a model of the discourses' dynamics.
  - Their operationalisation of the four positions (quoted from the paper): S1 "essence/identity", S2 "information/understanding", a "object cause of desire", $ "language-castrated subject"; e.g., Master: "S1 addresses S2 as holder of knowledge, driven by castrated subject ($)". Note they include the capitalist discourse, which is *not* a quarter-turn of the other four (it swaps $ and S1 on the left side) — so a Z4 rotation model cannot generate it.
  - Rigor verdict: modest. It is a content-analysis/annotation study with a probability bookkeeping scheme; α ≈ 0.5–0.7 is "moderate" agreement; sample is tiny. But it is (as far as this search found) the **first peer-reviewed empirical quantification of the Lacanian discourses**, and it is the mandatory predecessor citation for any MPN claim about detecting or scoring discourses in text.
- Graph-neural-network or agent-based simulations of the four discourses or the signifying chain: **none found** in this search (Frontiers, arXiv-indexed results, or Google-visible results). The nearest things are (a) Murtagh's hierarchical-clustering text analyses (Matte Blanco line, above), (b) the Seminar II automaton, (c) Iurato's group-theoretic reading of the mathemes (analogical). The Z4 cyclic-rotation description of the four discourses is Lacan's own (the "quarter turn" in *Seminar XVII*, 1969–70; Norton 2007) and is standard in the commentary (e.g., Bracher et al. eds. 1994, *Lacanian Theory of Discourse*, NYU Press; Verhaeghe 1995, "From Impossibility to Inability: Lacan's Theory of the Four Discourses", *The Letter* 3 — both UNVERIFIED). Stating it as a cyclic group is a re-description, not a discovery.
- **Possati (2020)**, "Algorithmic unconscious: why psychoanalysis helps in understanding AI", *Palgrave Communications* 6:70 — VERIFIED title (ResearchGate record); DOI 10.1057/s41599-020-0445-0 UNVERIFIED. **Possati (2021)**, *The Algorithmic Unconscious: How Psychoanalysis Helps in Understanding AI*, Routledge (Antinomies series), ISBN 978-0367694043, 156 pp. — VERIFIED (Routledge, review record). Possati uses Lacan (mirror stage, symbolic/imaginary, the "algorithmic unconscious" as the projective identification of humans with machines) plus Latour and Simondon; it is philosophy of technology, with no formal model, no code, no data. Related humanities works (UNVERIFIED): Isabel Millar (2021), *The Psychoanalysis of Artificial Intelligence*, Palgrave; Magee, Arora & Munn (2023), "Structured like a language model: Analysing AI as an automated subject", *Big Data & Society*. These are the right citations for "Lacan as a lens on AI", and they are all non-computational.
- "Computational psychoanalysis" as a term: Iurato 2018 (book title, VERIFIED); Tomic 2020 (arXiv, "Quantum Computational Psychoanalysis"); Joncas 2021 (blog). No "Tandon" reference surfaced in this search — UNVERIFIED/possibly non-existent; do not cite without locating.

### 5. Formal / topological Lacan

- **Greenshields (2017)**, *Writing the Structures of the Subject: Lacan and Topology*, Palgrave Macmillan (The Palgrave Lacan Series), DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-47533-2 — VERIFIED (Springer, PhilPapers). A literary/theoretical study of how Lacan *writes* with Möbius strip, torus, cross-cap, Klein bottle, Borromean knots; it does not attempt or claim mathematical proofs.
- **Ragland & Milovanovic (eds.) (2004)**, *Lacan: Topologically Speaking*, Other Press, ISBN 9781892746764 — VERIFIED (Other Press). Edited collection (contributors include Vappereau, Milovanovic, Ragland, Skriabine, Darmon...). Mixed: some chapters describe actual surfaces and knots correctly, most use them as figures.
- **Burgoyne (ed.) (2000)**, *Drawing the Soul: Schemas and Models in Psychoanalysis*, Rebus Press — UNVERIFIED (from memory). Contains Burgoyne's own "Autism and topology" and papers on the L-schema, graph of desire, etc.
- **Vappereau** — Jean-Michel Vappereau, *Étoffe: les surfaces topologiques intrinsèques* (Topologie en Extension, 1988); *Nœud: la théorie du nœud esquissée par J. Lacan* (1997); *Essaim: le groupe fondamental du nœud* (1985) — UNVERIFIED titles/years (from memory). Vappereau was one of Lacan's mathematical interlocutors (with Pierre Soury and Michel Thomé) and his books contain real low-dimensional topology (surface classification, fundamental groups of knot complements, Borromean and generalised Borromean links). Where they state theorems, the theorems are checkable knot theory; the *clinical* interpretation attached to each is not.
- Which claims are mathematically checkable? (a) The Borromean property of the three-ring link (no two rings linked, all three inseparable) and its generalisations to 4-component links (the "sinthome" as fourth ring) are standard knot theory — checkable, and true. (b) Surface statements (Möbius strip one-sided, cross-cap = projective plane with a disc removed, torus fundamental group Z×Z, Klein bottle non-orientable) — checkable, true, and Lacan's diagrams of them are mostly correct. (c) Claims of the form "the subject *is* a cross-cap", "desire *is* a torus meridian", "the Real *is* one ring" — not propositions in mathematics; nothing to check. (d) The "graph of desire" and L/R-schemas are diagrams, not graphs in the graph-theoretic sense (no defined edge semantics). So: the topology is real but its role is notational/metaphorical; no theorem about the psyche follows from any theorem about surfaces or knots. This is exactly the distinction the treatises must draw.

### 6. Is Lacan formalisable at all? The critical literature

- **Fink (2004)**, *Lacan to the Letter: Reading Écrits Closely*, University of Minnesota Press — UNVERIFIED (well known). Fink treats mathemes as "written" formulas meant to be transmitted "integrally" without commentary, but reads them via close reading; he does not treat them as algebra.
- **Milner (1995)**, *L'Œuvre claire: Lacan, la science, la philosophie*, Seuil — UNVERIFIED (from memory). Milner's thesis: Lacan's "mathemes" are a bid for *integral transmissibility* modelled on Galilean science (Koyré), and the later Lacan (Seminar XX onward) abandons the "hyperstructural" programme; formalisation was an *ideal of transmission*, not a claim that the mathemes compute anything.
- **Badiou** — "Formulas of *L'Étourdit*" (*Lacanian Ink* 27, 2006) and Badiou & Roudinesco (2014), *Jacques Lacan, Past and Present*, Columbia UP — UNVERIFIED. Badiou reads Lacan's "formulas of sexuation" (∀x Φx / ∃x ¬Φx etc.) as a deliberate misuse of quantifier logic to write an "inconsistency" — i.e., the formulas are *not* well-formed first-order logic on purpose. Related: the *Cahiers pour l'Analyse* (1966–69) programme — Miller's "Suture" and Badiou's "Mark and Lack" — reconstructed in Hallward & Peden (eds.) (2012), *Concept and Form* (2 vols., Verso) — UNVERIFIED.
- **Sokal & Bricmont (1998)**, *Fashionable Nonsense / Intellectual Impostures* — already known to the caller; their chapter on Lacan attacks the "erectile organ = √−1" passage and the topology as decorative. Note: their critique is that Lacan's mathematics is *irrelevant*, not that it is wrong; on the Borromean knot they concede the mathematics is correct.
- Practical consensus: (i) the mathemes are *ideograms* with a fixed reading grammar, not terms of a formal calculus; (ii) the only strictly formal fragments in Lacan are the α β γ δ automaton (Seminar II/Écrits), the logical-time sophism (the three prisoners), the quantifier formulas of sexuation (deliberately ill-formed), and the knot/surface topology (correct but interpretive); (iii) any model that assigns *numbers* to S1, S2, $, a is therefore a new construction owned by its author, not a translation of Lacan.

### 7. Mathematical psychology's own toolbox (what engineers actually reach for)

- **Borsboom (2017)**, "A network theory of mental disorders", *World Psychiatry* 16(1):5–13, DOI 10.1002/wps.20375, PMID 28127906 — VERIFIED (Wiley DOI page, PubMed). Mental disorders as stable states of symptom networks (symptoms causally coupled; "hysteresis"; strongly connected networks show early-warning signs and alternative stable states). Empirical toolbox: Gaussian graphical models / mixed graphical models on cross-sectional and time-series symptom data (bootnet, qgraph, mgm, graphicalVAR), with well-known replicability/stability critiques (Forbes et al. 2017; Borsboom et al. 2017 replies) — UNVERIFIED.
- Dynamical-systems clinical psychology: Hayes, Laurenceau, Feldman, Strauss & Cardaciotto (2007), "Change is not always linear: The study of nonlinear and discontinuous patterns of change in psychotherapy", *Clinical Psychology Review* 27(6):715–723 — UNVERIFIED; Hayes & Andrews (2020), "A complex systems approach to the study of change in psychotherapy", *BMC Medicine* 18:197 — UNVERIFIED; Schiepek's synergetics programme (Schiepek et al., "Synergetic Navigation System", *Frontiers in Psychology* 2016 and others) — UNVERIFIED; Olthof, Hasselman, Lichtwarck-Aschoff et al. on critical fluctuations preceding transitions in therapy (2020, *Clinical Psychological Science*) — UNVERIFIED.
- Catastrophe models of attitudes and behaviour: Zeeman (1976), "Catastrophe theory", *Scientific American* 234(4):65–83 — UNVERIFIED; Flay (1978) on attitude change as cusp — UNVERIFIED; van der Maas, Kolstein & van der Pligt (2003), "Sudden transitions in attitudes", *Sociological Methods & Research* 32(2):125–152 — UNVERIFIED; Guastello (1982 onward; Guastello, Koopmans & Pincus eds. 2009, *Chaos and Complexity in Psychology*, Cambridge UP) — UNVERIFIED. The standard *fitting* tools: Cobb's stochastic catastrophe theory (Cobb & Watson 1980, *Mathematical Modelling*; Cobb & Zacks 1985, *JASA*) and Grasman, van der Maas & Wagenmakers (2009), "Fitting the cusp catastrophe in R: A cusp package", *Journal of Statistical Software* 32(8) — UNVERIFIED. **Directly relevant to the corpus's Kramers = cusp observation:** Cobb's stochastic cusp is *by definition* the overdamped Langevin SDE dx = −V′(x)dt + σ dW with the quartic cusp potential V, whose stationary density is ∝ exp(−2V/σ²); the equivalence of "cusp with additive noise" and "Kramers escape over a quartic barrier" is therefore a textbook consequence of Cobb (1978/1981) and of Wagenmakers, Molenaar, Grasman, Hartelman & van der Maas (2005), "Transformation invariance in stochastic catastrophe theory", *Physica D* 211:263–276 (UNVERIFIED). The treatises should cite these rather than presenting the identification as new.
- Psychometrics: Big Five/OCEAN is the validated factor model (Costa & McCrae NEO; Goldberg IPIP); DISC (Marston 1928; commercial instruments) has weak peer-reviewed psychometric support and is generally not used in academic personality research. No prior "DISC⊗OCEAN tensor" was found; the nearest legitimate precedents are (a) multi-trait multi-method matrices (Campbell & Fiske 1959), (b) circumplex models (Wiggins' interpersonal circumplex; the two DISC axes are close to the dominance/affiliation circumplex axes), and (c) bifactor/hierarchical models of personality (DeYoung's Big Two: Stability/Plasticity). A tensor product of a 4-type commercial typology with a 5-factor model has no construct-validity basis in the literature; it should be presented as an engineering feature space, not a psychometric construct.

## What an engineering treatise can legitimately say

- **Cite predecessors, not novelty, for every bridge you use.** Mandatory predecessor citations for the MPN treatises: Carhart-Harris & Friston 2010 (*Brain*) and Solms 2019 (*Front. Psychol.*) for any "psychoanalysis ↔ free energy" language; Lacan Seminar II / Écrits "Purloined Letter" postface (and Liu 2010) for any "signifying chain as automaton / information-theoretic object"; Gadalla, Nikoletseas & Amazonas 2026 (*Front. Psychol.*, doi 10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1526215) for any claim about detecting or quantifying the four (five) discourses in text; Iurato 2018 for the term "computational psychoanalysis" and Matte Blanco 1975 / Lauro-Grotto 2008 / Murtagh 2012 for any "unconscious as set/metric structure" idea; Borsboom 2017 and the Cobb/van der Maas/Grasman cusp literature for symptom-network and catastrophe machinery.
- **Do not call the Kramers/cusp potential "free energy" without qualification.** In the FEP literature "free energy" is a variational bound on surprise; in the corpus it is a Landau-type potential in an overdamped SDE. Say "Landau/cusp potential (Cobb's stochastic cusp; equivalently a Kramers escape problem)" and, if invoking Friston, state that the two are different quantities.
- **The Z4 quarter-turn is Lacan's own description** (Seminar XVII); write "we encode Lacan's quarter-turn as the cyclic group Z4 acting on the tuple (S1, S2, $, a)" and note that the capitalist discourse is *not* in the orbit (Gadalla et al. treat it as a fifth discourse). Any dynamics on that group (transition rates, operator breakdown) is the corpus's own construction and should be labelled a hypothesis.
- **Numerical assignment to S1/S2/$/a has no precedent in the Lacanian literature and no formal warrant in Lacan** (Milner, Badiou, Fink on mathemes as ideograms; Sokal-Bricmont). The honest framing: "We treat the four terms as *roles* in a labelled structure and attach engineering semantics to them; no claim is made that this is what Lacan's mathemes denote."
- **Topology claims must be split into (a) checkable mathematics and (b) interpretation.** Borromean links and non-orientable surfaces are correct mathematics (Vappereau; Ragland & Milovanovic 2004; Greenshields 2017 for the literary reading); nothing about subjectivity follows from them as theorems. If the treatises use a knot or surface, either prove a property of the *model* or state that the figure is notational.
- **The strongest existing empirical bridge is annotation + reliability (Gadalla et al. 2026, α ≈ 0.5–0.7 on 264 sentences).** Any MPN claim to "score" discourses should be benchmarked against, or at least compared with, that method; and since the codebase has no learned model, the treatises must not describe discourse detection as implemented.
- **The DISC⊗OCEAN "psychometric tensor" should be described as an engineering feature space**, with an explicit note that DISC lacks academic validation and that no tensor-product construct exists in the psychometric literature; cite circumplex and hierarchical (Big Two) models as the nearest validated relatives.
- **Prefer the validated toolbox for anything empirical:** network psychometrics (Borsboom 2017 + bootnet/graphicalVAR), stochastic cusp fitting (Grasman et al. 2009), and early-warning-signal analysis (Hayes/Olthof/Schiepek), each of which comes with published estimation and reliability procedures — unlike any Lacanian formalisation.
