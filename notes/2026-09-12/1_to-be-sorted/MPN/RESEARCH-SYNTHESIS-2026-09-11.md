## Deep Dive: MPN treatise foundations (Lacan formalisation, sonification science, alarm standards, psychometrics, music-theory formalisms, stochastic dynamics, generative-music ML, computational psychoanalysis, EU law)

Synthesis of nine research files (q1 to q9, 2026-09-11) and sections 0 and 9 of the MPN Conductor v3.7 repository deconstruction, prepared for the Eigenia research corpus (eigenia.nl), working group WG-09. Verification labels follow the source files: VERIFIED means a bibliographic record or full text was seen in the research session; UNVERIFIED means the item was cited from working knowledge and still needs a check before print.

### Executive Summary

The single most consequential finding is legal: Article 5(1)(f) of the EU AI Act has prohibited, since 2 February 2025, any AI system that infers the emotions of a natural person in the workplace from biometric data, and the Commission's guidelines read "emotion" broadly enough to include arousal, stress-based anxiety and team-level "emotional tone", so an MPN that renders operator affect from voice, HRV, gaze or EEG is prohibited by construction and the design must instead sonify plant state, alarm load, interaction tempo and, at most, schedule-derived fatigue (European Parliament and Council 2024; European Commission 2025). The second finding is that the codebase behind the series is a rule-based prototype: the only psychology-to-music mapping that runs is a set of hand-written thresholds and linear maps over nine scalars, the PSYCHOSCORE "trained models" learned nothing (random targets, ten error-derived labels, a placeholder training loop), the 57-dimensional vector is projected and then discarded, and no listening test, dataset or validation exists, so the treatises may describe a sonification design and prototype but not an AI system (repo-deconstruction sections 0 and 9). Third, the musical mappings that have real evidence are few and specific: tempo and event rate to arousal is the most robust and most culturally stable cue, mode to valence holds for Western-enculturated listeners and is dominated by tempo when the two conflict, roughness and harmonic distance predict continuous tension ratings as components, and timbre brightness tracks arousal, whereas instrument-family affect, neo-Riemannian operation affect and metre-as-anxiety are craft or hermeneutic claims without controlled data (Juslin and Laukka 2003; Lerdahl and Krumhansl 2007; Farbood 2012; Eerola, Ferrer and Alluri 2012). Fourth, the auditory channel of a control room is already a regulated resource governed by ISA-18.2, IEC 62682:2022 and EEMUA 191 edition 4, and the best-evidenced continuous sonification results come from anaesthesia, where sound is as good as vision for detection but worse for diagnosis, with no controlled control-room study found at all (Loeb and Fitch 2002; Watson and Sanderson 2004; IEC 2022). Fifth, the theoretical apparatus survives only as clearly labelled analogy: Lacan's four discourses are a finite combinatorial object under quarter-turn rotation, the stochastic cusp and Kramers escape are the same overdamped SDE and no one has fitted either to a human operator, DISC lacks independent validity, the DISC-by-OCEAN tensor has no psychometric precedent, and the "free energy" of the corpus is a Landau potential and not Friston's variational bound (Crans, Fiore and Satyendra 2009; Grasman, van der Maas and Wagenmakers 2009; Hänggi, Talkner and Borkovec 1990; Solms 2019).

### Findings

#### 1. Lacan's formal apparatus: what is citable and what is metaphor

Lacan's mathemes S1, S2, $ and a, the four discourses, the RSI Borromean knot, the graph of desire and the formulas of sexuation are all documented in standard editions, but almost every page reference remains UNVERIFIED because no publisher page was fetched within budget (Lacan 1969-70/2007; Lacan 1972-73/1998; Lacan 1966/2006; Fink 1995; Evans 1996). The one fully verified primary item is Miller's "Suture", first delivered at Seminar XII in February 1965, published in Cahiers pour l'Analyse 1(3) in 1966 and in English in Screen 18(4) in 1977-78; Miller reads Frege's construction of zero from the concept "not identical with itself" as the mark of a lack the numerical chain both depends on and effaces, a philosophical reading rather than a mathematical result, and one that Badiou contests (Miller 1966; Miller 1977-78; Badiou 1990/2008). The four discourses are a finite, exactly specifiable structure: four terms in four fixed slots (agent, other, truth, product), with each discourse a quarter-turn of the previous, so that only the four cyclic rotations are Lacanian discourses and the "capitalist discourse", which swaps S1 and $ on the left, falls outside the orbit (Lacan 1969-70/2007; Fink 1995; Bracher 1993; nosubject.com n.d.). Representing this as the cyclic group Z4 acting on the tuple is faithful to Lacan's own "quarter turn", but any transition probabilities, state-machine semantics or numerical values attached to the letters are the treatise's own construction, since the Lacanian literature contains no such semantics (Verhaeghe 1995; Clemens and Grigg 2006).

Sokal and Bricmont's specific objections stand unrefuted on the mathematics: the compactness passage in Seminar XX garbles the definition, the equation of the erectile organ with the square root of minus one is ornament, and the topological analogies never say which properties carry over (Sokal and Bricmont 1998). The serious responses concede the literal content and defend analogical or rhetorical use (Plotnitsky 2002; Fink 2004), or assert that the topology is a non-metaphorical "writing" of structure, a position Sokal and Bricmont would call unfalsifiable (Ragland and Milovanovic 2004; Burgoyne 2000; Glynos and Stavrakakis 2002). The synthesis must flag that these two defences contradict each other and cannot be cited together in support of a single claim. The Borromean property itself is correct knot theory (a three-component Brunnian link: cut any ring and the other two separate) and can serve as a structural-constraint metaphor for three coupled registers with no pairwise binding, provided the treatise says so and extracts no quantitative prediction from it (Wikipedia n.d., Borromean clinic; Lacan 1974-75; Lacan 1975-76/2016).

Lacan's Seminar II engagement with cybernetics is real and documented: the plus/minus game and the alpha-beta-gamma-delta network show that a syntax alone produces forbidden successions, that is memory and law without a subject, and the 22 June 1955 lecture treats the machine as the symbolic order detached from the subject (Lacan 1954-55/1988; Liu 2010b; Geoghegan 2011; Geoghegan 2023). This is a legitimate historical precedent for reading the symbolic order as a finite-state syntax with memory constraints, but claiming that Lacan anticipated Markov models or control theory in a technical sense is a misreading. The formulas of sexuation are written by Lacan in quantifier notation, yet the "not-all" negation is deliberately non-classical, so transcribing them as first-order logic is also a misreading (Copjec 1994; Chiesa 2016; Le Gaufey 2006; Zupančič 2017).

The peer-reviewed computational field is essentially one research group. Gadalla, Nikoletseas and Amazonas annotated 40 DailyDialog dialogues (264 sentences) with discourse and emotion labels and proposed a "Lacanian Discourse Discovery" pipeline, stating that their 2023 paper was the first systematic combination of computational methods with psychoanalysis (Gadalla, Nikoletseas and Amazonas 2026; Gadalla, Nikoletseas and Amazonas 2023). No peer-reviewed mathematical or computational model of RSI was found at all; the rest of the field is interpretive application (Parker 2005; Applying Lacan's four discourse model 2022; Reading Balint group work 2016). The defensible framing for a control-room text is that Lacan supplies a discrete vocabulary (four positions, four terms, a rotation rule) for describing who is put to work by whom in a socio-technical link, adopted as an engineering ontology without inheriting its metapsychological claims, which is how Bracher's cultural criticism also proceeds (Bracher 1993; Bracher et al. 1994).

#### 2. Sonification science: definitions, mapping evidence and the anaesthesia base

Sonification is an established field with an NSF-commissioned status report, an open-access handbook and a stable technique taxonomy of audification, parameter-mapping sonification, model-based sonification and earcons or auditory icons (Kramer et al. 1999; Hermann, Hunt and Neuhoff 2011; Walker and Nees 2011; Grond and Berger 2011). Hermann's four-part definition (objective data relation, systematic transformation, reproducibility, generality) is the standard one but was paraphrased from memory and must not be quoted (Hermann 2008). Mapping choices are empirical, not conventional: Walker's magnitude-estimation programme shows that polarity (does rising pitch mean more or less?) and scaling depend on the data concept and on the listener population, so mappings must be validated with the intended operators rather than assumed (Walker 2002; Georgia Tech repository n.d.).

The strongest continuous-sonification evidence comes from anaesthesia. Loeb and Fitch had fourteen residents monitor six physiological variables carried in two rhythmic streams (a heartbeat-triggered cardiovascular sound and a breath-triggered respiratory sound); every subject detected every event in every format, detection was fastest with the combined display (10.4 s) versus visual (12.8 s) and auditory-only (13.0 s), but identification accuracy fell to 60 percent for audio alone against 88 percent visual and 80 percent combined (Loeb and Fitch 2002). Watson and Sanderson showed that a continuous respiratory sonification supports eyes-free monitoring and task time-sharing alongside the pulse-oximeter tone, although effect sizes were not retrieved (Watson and Sanderson 2004; Sanderson, Watson and Russell 2005; Sanderson 2006). The pulse-oximeter variable-pitch tone is the single field-proven continuous sonification, and even it conveys trend well but absolute level poorly; the Queensland group's laboratory work shows that adding tremolo and brightness at threshold crossings improves range identification (Paterson et al. 2016; Schwarz and Sanderson 2017). The design lesson is sound for detection and orientation, vision for diagnosis.

Ambient or soundscape monitoring of technical systems has a plausible perceptual rationale and no controlled performance data. Peep mapped events to bird chirps, states to waterfall and wind intensity and liveness to cricket chirps, on the argument that natural sounds combine pleasingly and that deviation from a familiar background is noticed at low cost, but it reports no quantitative evaluation (Gilfix and Couch 2000). Vickers' handbook chapter catalogues Peep, WebMelody and program-debugging sonifications and endorses the soundscape direction without any summary of concurrent-stream limits or annoyance thresholds (Vickers 2011; Barra et al. 2002). SOC sonification work is attitude research rather than performance research (Axon et al. 2018). Concurrent-stream capacity is bounded by auditory scene analysis, streams must be segregated by timbre, register, rhythm or location, and demonstrated designs carry up to six variables in two streams, so the treatise should state a design rule ("few, well-segregated streams; backgrounds whose absence is salient") rather than a capacity number (Bregman 1990; Fitch and Kramer 1994).

The standards landscape is entirely about discrete alarms. Alarm fatigue and masking are documented clinically, IEC 60601-1-8 was amended in 2020 with auditory-icon-style sounds after Edworthy's research showed the 2006 melodic alarms were hard to learn, and ISO 7731 sets audibility-over-noise requirements for workplace danger signals (Sendelbach and Funk 2013; Momtahan, Hétu and Tansley 1993; IEC 60601-1-8; ISO 7731:2003; ISO 24500:2010). No ISO or IEC standard covers continuous, non-alarm sonification of process state, and no controlled field or simulator study of continuous sonification in an industrial control room was found; every performance claim in the series transfers from anaesthesia or the laboratory and must be labelled as such.

#### 3. Alarm-management standards and control-room human factors

The auditory channel of a control room is regulated. ISA-18.2 was issued in 2009 and revised in 2016; IEC 62682 edition 1 (2014) was adapted from ISA-18.2-2009 and edition 2 (2022-12-08, 168 pages, TC 65/SC 65A) incorporates changes from ISA-18.2-2016, with stated alignment to EEMUA 191 and NAMUR NA 102 (IEC 2022; ISA 2016). EEMUA 191 was first published in 1999 with HSE endorsement after the Milford Haven explosion, reached edition 3 in 2013 and edition 4 in November 2024, so citing edition 3 as current would be out of date (EEMUA 2013; EEMUA 2024; IChemE Hazards 35 2025). The lifecycle framework means any new auditory channel must be declared in the site alarm philosophy, rationalised so it is not itself an alarm unless it demands an operator response, and brought under monitoring and management of change (IEC 2022).

The benchmark figures widely attributed to ISA-18.2 could not be seen in the standard itself, which is paywalled; only the endpoints are verified in the primary literature, namely HSE's guidance, citing EEMUA 191, that the long-term average rate should be no more than one alarm every ten minutes and no more than ten displayed in the first ten minutes after a major upset, with a 5/15/80 percent high/medium/low priority split (Health and Safety Executive 2000). A separate Perplexity cross-check found the fuller ISA-18.2 table reproduced in public vendor and HSE-adjacent sources: roughly 150 annunciated alarms per operator per day as very likely acceptable and 300 per day as maximum manageable; about 1 versus 2 alarms per ten minutes as the acceptable versus manageable average; a peak of at most 10 alarms in any ten-minute period; less than 1 percent of time in flood; the top ten most frequent alarms contributing less than 1 to 5 percent of total load; fewer than 5 stale alarms per day; chattering defined on a first pass as 3 or more transitions per minute; and a target of zero chattering or fleeting alarms. These figures are attributed to the public reproductions, namely the exida journey paper (https://www.exida.com/images/uploads/Alarm_Management_and_ISA_18_2-_A_journey_not_a_destination.pdf), the Siemens white paper (https://cache.industry.siemens.com/dl/files/836/109772836/att_1002094/v1/WP_Alarm_Management_ISA_18.pdf), the archived ISA-18.2-2009 PDF (https://ia800809.us.archive.org/12/items/RP120601/18.2.pdf), Kim Van Camp's ISA Ireland slides (https://isa.ie/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/Alarm_System_Performance_Metrics_Kim_Van_camp.pdf), an Octave document (https://media.ffycdn.net/us/octave/d/12c802d393ab446dbb6dd82dd1ebd070) and exida's alarm-philosophy page (https://www.exida.com/Alarm-Management/Detail/alarm_philosophy); they lie outside the nine research files and are therefore not carried into the Sources list below. IEC 62682:2022 Table 5 ("Average alarm rates", page 83) and Table 7 ("Recommended alarm performance metrics summary", page 87) are verified to exist from the table of contents but their contents are not publicly reproduced anywhere found (IEC 2022).

Alarm floods are causally implicated in real accidents. At Milford Haven on 24 July 1994 the two operators had to recognise, acknowledge and act on 275 alarms in the last eleven minutes before the explosion, and HSE found that the excessive number of alarms reduced the effectiveness of operator response and that graphics lacked a process overview; 26 people were injured and damage was about 48 million pounds (Health and Safety Executive 1997; Health and Safety Executive 2000; Bransby and Jenkinson 1997). The BP Texas City report was released on 20 March 2007 and its fatigue and staffing specifics are widely repeated but were not seen on a CSB page; Three Mile Island and the NRC's NUREG-0700 and NUREG-0711 programme are canonical but were not fetched (U.S. CSB 2007; Kemeny Commission 1979; U.S. NRC n.d.). The design consequence is that any added auditory stream must degrade gracefully, preferably to silence, during floods, which is precisely when an operator-state signal would be most informative and least usable.

The validation stack for a new modality is established practice. Endsley's three-level situation-awareness model and SAGAT freeze probes supply the criterion, ISO 11064 parts 1 and 7 and ISO 9241-210 supply the human-centred design and evaluation process, and NASA-TLX supplies the accepted subjective workload measure (Endsley 1995; Endsley 1988; ISO 11064 n.d.; Hart and Staveland 1988). Wickens' multiple resource theory gives a principled reason to prefer auditory over further visual presentation for a visually saturated operator, but it also predicts interference with alarm horns and crew speech, so the treatise can claim reduced cross-modal cost, not zero cost, and should distinguish non-verbal sonification from spoken messages (Wickens 2002; Wickens 2008). Rasmussen's skill-rule-knowledge taxonomy and Reason's error taxonomy describe how operators respond to process signals; Atkinson's Hazards paper on the Alarm Usefulness Questionnaire contains no quantitative response-time data (Rasmussen 1983; Reason 1990; Atkinson 2015). Real-time physiological workload and fatigue sensing (HRV, pupillometry, blink rate, PERCLOS, eye tracking) is an active published area in nuclear main control rooms, used offline in integrated system validation, in simulator research and in fatigue detection; no source was found describing physiological or psychological state fed back to the crew in real time by any modality (Int. J. Industrial Ergonomics 2020; Sensors 2024; Applied Sciences 2023; ASCE n.d.; Idaho National Laboratory n.d.; Frontiers in Neuroergonomics 2025; Fernandes et al. 2017). Any such sensor also creates a new class of sensitive personal data inside the IACS boundary that falls under the asset owner's security programme (IEC 2024).

#### 4. Psychometrics: traits, states and what can be measured in real time

The Five-Factor Model is the mainstream, replicated and stable taxonomy of trait personality, converging from lexical and questionnaire traditions and replicating well in literate industrialised samples with translated instruments, less well in indigenous lexical studies (Goldberg 1990; Goldberg 1993; Costa and McCrae 1992; John and Srivastava 1999; McCrae and Costa 1997; Piedmont and Chae 1997). Block's contrarian critique that five is not compelled by the data and that the model is descriptive rather than explanatory still stands, and the model is a taxonomy of between-person differences, not a state model, so it cannot tell what an operator is doing at 03:14 (Block 1995). No reliability coefficient was seen on a fetched page and none should be quoted without the manual or a meta-analysis. DISC descends from Marston's 1928 typology through Clarke and Geier to commercial instruments; the scientific validity of DISC has not been demonstrated, its dimensions are not psychometrically independent and are better explained by Big Five Extraversion and Agreeableness, and the validation evidence that exists is publisher technical reports rather than independent peer review, so DISC may be used only as a lay communication vocabulary (Wikipedia n.d., DISC assessment; Marston 1928).

The Dark Tetrad and its Short Dark Tetrad instrument are well-defined research constructs. The SD4 has 28 items in four seven-item subscales, was developed on samples of 868, 999 and 660, and reports Study 3 alphas of .80 (narcissism), .75 (Machiavellianism), .81 (psychopathy) and .81 (sadism), with sadism, psychopathy and Machiavellianism correlating negatively with Agreeableness and narcissism positively with Extraversion (Paulhus et al. 2021; Paulhus and Williams 2002; Buckels, Jones and Paulhus 2013; Paulhus 2014). These are trait measures of socially aversive personality validated for research on between-person differences; using them as operational risk flags for individual operators is an unvalidated extrapolation. A search for tensor or Kronecker products of two trait inventories returned only mathematics pages: no psychometric literature forms such a product, DISC would be heavily redundant with the Big Five inside it, and the legitimate alternatives are joint factor analysis, circumplex models, hierarchical models and HEXACO, which absorbs much dark-trait variance through Honesty-Humility (Hofstee, de Raad and Goldberg 1992; Ashton and Lee 2007). Any DISC-by-OCEAN tensor must be presented as an engineering parameterisation with no measurement-theoretic warrant.

The state-trait distinction, canonically operationalised in the STAI, means that a trait score administered once is at most a prior on the base rate of states, not a real-time reading (Spielberger et al. 1983). The legitimate real-time channels index arousal and cognitive load, each with named confounds: HRV (RMSSD and HF fall under acute mental stress; LF/HF as sympathovagal balance is disputed; respiration, posture, caffeine and circadian phase confound it) (Task Force of the ESC and NASPE 1996; Kim et al. 2018); electrodermal activity, which indexes sympathetic arousal without valence (Boucsein 2012); task-evoked pupil dilation, which is swamped by screen luminance and gaze angle in a control room (Kahneman and Beatty 1966; Beatty 1982; Winn et al. 2018); eye-tracking and EEG frontal-theta and parietal-alpha indices, which suffer from artefacts and poor cross-session generalisation (Hogervorst, Brouwer and van Erp 2014); and speech-based stress recognition, whose accuracies are corpus-specific and collapse on spontaneous, noisy, cross-corpus data (Measuring negative emotions and stress through acoustic correlates in speech 2025; An evaluation of speech-based recognition of emotional and physiological markers of stress 2021). None of these measures emotion, mood, morale or trust.

Barrett and colleagues' review is the decisive scientific caution and, as theme 9 shows, the legal one: no facial configuration was found to be distinctive of a particular emotion across cultures, a smile can signal submission rather than happiness, and facial movements are "not universally diagnostic of emotional states", with the argument transferring directly to vocal expression (Barrett et al. 2019). Professional standards reinforce the point that validity attaches to an interpretation for a use, so re-purposing a trait test or a physiological index as a fitness-for-duty signal is a new use requiring its own evidence, under conditions of meaningful consent, purpose limitation and contestability (AERA, APA and NCME 2014; APA 2017; International Test Commission 2001; Evers et al. 2013). Aggregating individual physiology into a "team psychological state" is an untested inferential leap.

#### 5. Music-theory formalisms and music-cognition evidence

Neo-Riemannian theory provides a rigorous discrete model of moves between states. Lewin's generalized interval systems turn intervals into group actions on a set of musical objects, and Cohn's P, L and R operations on the 24 consonant triads are involutions that preserve two common tones and move one voice by a semitone or a whole tone (Lewin 1987; Cohn 1996; Cohn 1997; Cohn 1998; Cohn 2012). Crans, Fiore and Satyendra prove that the PLR group is the dihedral group of order 24, acting simply transitively on the triads and dual to the transposition-inversion group; alternating P and L yields six-cycles (hexatonic systems), P and R eight-cycles (octatonic) and L and R a 24-cycle, which is a clean citable way to define closed loops of different periods in a state space (Crans, Fiore and Satyendra 2009; Douthett and Steinbach 1998; Tymoczko 2011). What the theory does not give is any empirical claim about what those moves feel like; Cohn's "uncanny" hexatonic poles are hermeneutic (Cohn 2004). The repository's mpn_engine implements a wrong L operator enshrined by a test, so the treatises must state which implementation they use (repo-deconstruction section 9).

Tension and key are empirically modelled within the Western tonal idiom. Lerdahl's pitch-space distances feed a hierarchical tension model that, combined with attraction and dissonance terms, fits listeners' continuous tension ratings better than a sequential model, though the coefficients were not seen (Lerdahl 2001; Lerdahl and Krumhansl 2007; Bigand, Parncutt and Lerdahl 1996). Farbood's parametric temporal model is the most reusable engineering formulation because it is explicitly multi-feature (harmonic tension, pitch height, loudness, onset rate) and time-windowed, and it has been automated (Farbood 2012; TenseMusic 2024). The Krumhansl-Schmuckler key-finding algorithm is a linear correlation classifier over a twelve-vector of pitch-class durations, easy to reproduce and cite (Krumhansl and Kessler 1982; Krumhansl 1990; Temperley 1999). Roughness is a psychoacoustic quantity of a timbre-interval pair rather than of an interval alone, and consonance preference is culturally shaped, so the treatise must separate roughness from valence (Plomp and Levelt 1965; Sethares 1993; Sethares 2005; McDermott, Lehr and Oxenham 2010; McDermott et al. 2016). Huron's ITPRA theory supplies vocabulary for tension as anticipatory arousal, not a fitted model (Huron 2006).

The evidence on structure-to-emotion mappings is asymmetric. Tempo is the single most consistent acoustic correlate of perceived arousal in both music and speech across a meta-analysis of 104 vocal-expression and 41 music-performance studies, whose shared cues are rate, intensity, high-frequency energy, pitch level and articulation (Juslin and Laukka 2003). Mode maps to valence for Western-enculturated listeners, tempo dominates mode when they conflict, and cross-cultural work shows tempo cues are recognised by listeners without Western exposure while mode is less universal (Hevner 1936; Hevner 1937; Gabrielsson and Lindström 2010; Husain, Thompson and Schellenberg 2002; Fritz et al. 2009; Balkwill and Thompson 1999). Dimensional valence-arousal models dominate the field's 251 reviewed studies, and perceived emotion (recognising what music expresses) is far better established than induced emotion, so a notation that communicates state relies only on the stronger claim (Eerola and Vuoskoski 2013; Eerola and Vuoskoski 2011; Juslin and Västfjäll 2008; Juslin 2013). Timbre brightness and attack time predict arousal and timbre alone shifts perceived emotion, whereas instrument-family affect from orchestration treatises is craft knowledge with no controlled evidence (Eerola, Ferrer and Alluri 2012; Hailstone et al. 2009; Grey 1977; McAdams et al. 1995; Adler and Rimsky-Korsakov n.d.; McAdams et al. 2018). Regular metre supports focused anticipatory attention by dynamic attending theory and entrainment limits, but "irregular metre equals anxiety" is an inference, not a finding (Lerdahl and Jackendoff 1983; London 2004; Jones and Boltz 1989; Large and Jones 1999).

There is precedent for formal process to score (Xenakis), for time-series data to musical parameters and occasionally to notation (the Accentus patent for musical sonification of financial data, whose figures render notation, and the CRAN musicXML package), and for system state to sound (Peep), but no prior work was found that uses staff notation itself as a psychometric or operational-state representation intended to be read (Xenakis 1992; Childs, Perkins and Brooks 2006; musicXML n.d.; Gilfix and Couch 2000; Ballora et al. 2004; Editorial: Sonification 2023). That gap is the treatise's claimed novelty and should be stated cautiously given the eight-query search.

#### 6. Stochastic dynamics: Kramers, the cusp, drift-diffusion, early warnings and team synchrony

Kramers' escape rate is a rigorous asymptotic result for a one-dimensional Langevin system with additive Gaussian white noise in the high-barrier limit, and the canonical review confirms the overdamped form k = (omega0 omegab / 2 pi gamma) exp(-Eb/kT) as its Eq. (1.6), with validity requiring the barrier to greatly exceed the noise and with transition-state theory seriously overestimating the true rate (Kramers 1940; Hänggi, Talkner and Borkovec 1990; Gardiner n.d.). The bridge to behaviour is that any overdamped diffusion in a potential obeys the same mean-first-passage-time formula with the noise intensity in place of kT, provided one can justify a scalar state, a well-and-barrier potential, additive white noise and a barrier much larger than the noise. Cobb's stochastic cusp, dY = (alpha + beta Y - Y^3) dt + dW, has the Boltzmann-type stationary density exp(-V/sigma^2), so the cusp gives the shape of the double well and Kramers gives the hopping rate between its minima; this equivalence is a textbook consequence of Cobb's work and of transformation-invariance results, and the treatises should cite it rather than present it as new (Grasman, van der Maas and Wagenmakers 2009; Cobb 1978-1981; Cobb and Watson 1980; Wagenmakers et al. 2005). The corpus's use of "free energy" for this potential is a Landau potential, not Friston's variational free energy, and the two must not be conflated (Solms 2019).

Catastrophe theory is established as a statistical model class and contested as a theory. Zahler and Sussmann's Nature critique found applied catastrophe theory "characterised by incorrect reasoning, far-fetched assumptions, erroneous consequences, and exaggerated claims", because gradient dynamics is rarely demonstrated and the catastrophe flags can arise from other mechanisms (Zahler and Sussmann 1977; Sussmann and Zahler 1978a; Sussmann and Zahler 1978b; Guckenheimer 1978; Thom 1972/1975; Zeeman 1976; Zeeman 1977). The modern standard, accepted by later applied workers, is that a cusp is asserted only after maximum-likelihood or polynomial-regression fitting beats linear and logistic alternatives with a priori control variables, which is what Cobb's method, the cusp package and Guastello's difference-equation method supply (Guastello 1988; Guastello 2013; Guastello and Gregson 2011; van der Maas and Molenaar 1992; Stewart and Peregoy 1983; Chen and Chen 2015; Catastrophe theory applications in clinical psychology n.d.). Guastello's cusps of occupational accidents, workload and fatigue are the closest published precedent for a cusp of operator performance.

The drift-diffusion model is the only stochastic-dynamics model in this list with decades of quantitative fits to human data, neural correlates and standard estimation software; it quantifies sleep-deprivation effects as lowered and more variable drift, and Bogacz and colleagues relate the leaky accumulator to the sequential probability ratio test and derive the optimal speed-accuracy trade-off (Ratcliff 1978; Ratcliff and McKoon 2008; Bogacz et al. 2006; Busemeyer and Townsend 1993; Usher and McClelland 2001; Wagenmakers, van der Maas and Grasman 2007; Ratcliff and Van Dongen 2011; Gold and Shadlen 2007). Early-warning signals of critical transitions (critical slowing down, rising lag-1 autocorrelation and variance, skewness, flickering) are a published cross-domain method already applied to human mood time series in experience-sampling cohorts and in a single-case tapering study, with the caveats that they are necessary rather than sufficient and need long stationary series (Scheffer et al. 2009; Scheffer et al. 2012; Dakos et al. 2012; Kuehn 2011; van de Leemput et al. 2014; Wichers, Groot and Psychosystems/ESM Group 2016; Elevated emotion network connectivity n.d.). Yerkes and Dodson never plotted a single inverted U; the "law" mutated into folklore and the human-factors replacement is Hancock and Warm's extended-U model of adaptive dynamic range (Yerkes and Dodson 1908; Teigen 1994; Corbett 2015; Hancock and Warm 1989).

The honest search result is that no paper fits a Kramers escape rate to human operator state, fatigue or attentional lapses. The nearest precedents are attractor-network and double-well models of single decisions and bistable perception, two-state and hidden-Markov treatments of lapses, and bistable flip-flop models of the sleep-wake switch whose noise-driven transitions are Kramers-like without citing Kramers (Moreno-Bote, Rinzel and Rubin 2007; Braun and Mattia 2010; Roxin and Ledberg 2008; Wang 2002; Esterman et al. 2013; Shelat, Schooler and Giesbrecht 2024; Saper, Scammell and Lu 2005; Phillips and Robinson 2007). Softmax temperature in choice rules is a Gibbs distribution, not a barrier-crossing rate. For teams, the Kuramoto order parameter is an analogy: the empirical literature on physiological compliance uses cross-correlation, coherence and recurrence measures rather than fitted coupling constants, and a critical review finds measurement heterogeneous and effect sizes inconsistent (Kuramoto 1975; Strogatz 2000; Acebrón et al. 2005; Henning, Boucsein and Gil 2001; Elkins et al. 2009; Gorman et al. n.d.; Gorman, Amazeen and Cooke 2010; Kazi et al. 2021). Fitting any of these to control-room data would need a densely sampled scalar state, a priori control variables, bimodality and hysteresis in the same data, surrogate-tested rolling autocorrelation, and an independent noise estimate; absent those, the models are illustrative.

#### 7. Generative-music ML: what conditioning on a psychometric vector would require

MusicGen is a single-stage autoregressive transformer over EnCodec tokens (32 kHz, 50 Hz frame rate, four codebooks of 2048 entries) with a delay interleaving pattern, text conditioning through cross-attention from T5, FLAN-T5 or CLAP encoders, and an unsupervised chroma pathway that is deliberately bottlenecked; it comes in 300M, 1.5B and 3.3B sizes, was trained on 20,000 hours of licensed music, and its 3.3B model scores FAD 3.8, KL 1.22, CLAP 0.31, OVL 84.81 plus or minus 0.95 and REL 82.47 plus or minus 1.25 on MusicCaps (Copet et al. 2023; Défossez et al. 2022). The chroma pathway proves that a non-text structured signal can be injected, but it was trained jointly on the full corpus; a 57-dimensional vector projected to 768 dimensions is a new conditioning pathway the base model has never seen and, as the repository analysis shows, the projector is dead code at inference (repo-deconstruction section 0). AudioLDM and AudioLDM 2 are the latent-diffusion alternatives whose CLAP-space conditioning is by construction a continuous vector (Liu et al. 2023a; Liu et al. 2023b).

LoRA fine-tuning of MusicGen is documented practice: the musicgen-dreamboothing repository adapts all five checkpoints on a 27-minute dataset in under fifteen minutes on an A100 at about 10 GB, with adapters of about 100 MB, but reports no FAD, CLAP or MOS metrics, and the 2025-26 papers on few-shot and genre-specific LoRA were confirmed only as listings (Hu et al. 2021; ylacombe n.d.; Instruct-MusicGen n.d.; Few-shot LoRA tuning n.d.; Enhancing Diffusion-Based Music Generation n.d.; Persian MusicGen n.d.). What can be claimed is feasibility of adaptation; what cannot is that any fine-tune has learned a controllable axis. Preference optimisation for audio has two published templates: MusicRL's RLHF on 300,000 pairwise human preferences from deployed MusicLM users, whose own conclusion is that text adherence and quality explain only part of preference, and Tango 2's diffusion-DPO on synthetically constructed pairs (Cideron et al. 2024; Majumder et al. 2024; Rafailov et al. 2023; Wallace et al. 2023). CLAP is a text-adherence score, and a psychometric vector has no native text, so using CLAP as reward means either verbalising the vector or training a new alignment head that itself needs data; listing-level evidence exists that automatic metrics track human preference imperfectly (Wu et al. 2023; Elizalde et al. 2022; Benchmarking Music Generation Models n.d.; Factual and Musical Evaluation Metrics n.d.; SongBench n.d.).

The emotion-conditioned symbolic literature sets the realistic scale. EMOPIA has 1,087 clips from 387 pop-piano songs (about 11 hours), labelled in four valence-arousal quadrants by four annotators, with a generation baseline evaluated by 25 participants rating twelve samples each on five-point Likert scales; VGMIDI, its predecessor, had 95 samples (Hung et al. 2021; Ferreira and Whitehead 2019; Zhang et al. 2018; Huang et al. 2018; Huang and Yang 2020; Dong et al. 2023; OpenAI 2019). No fetched source conditions any generator on more than two to four emotion dimensions, so a 57-dimensional continuous conditioning claim has no literature precedent to lean on, and the first credible milestone is two-dimensional or a handful of factor scores evaluated as in EMOPIA. The minimum bar before writing "a trained psychometric-to-music model" is a labelled corpus at EMOPIA scale, a held-out set of vectors, an objective metric independent of the training signal, a listening study of at least about 25 listeners with attention checks and loudness normalisation, a same-protocol baseline and confidence intervals; MUSHRA presupposes a hidden reference that vector-conditioned generation lacks, so absolute or pairwise rating is the appropriate form (Lechler et al. 2025).

Affective and physiology-driven generative music is an established field with surveys and recent physiological-coupling work, which the treatise can use for positioning while distinguishing questionnaire-derived psychometric state from the EEG, HRV and EDA signals that dominate that literature; note that Williams and colleagues' 2015 survey appeared in Psychology of Music, not Music Perception (Williams et al. 2015; Williams et al. 2017; Dash and Agres 2024; Cyborg synchrony 2025; Digital music interventions 2023; Development of a biofeedback system n.d.; On the use of AI for Generation of Functional Music 2020; Music generation based on emotional EEG n.d.; Neural Musical Instruments n.d.). Until the data exist, the defensible description of the current system is a rule-based mapping from a psychometric vector to a text prompt consumed by a pretrained MusicGen-small, with the LoRA, DPO and CLAP stack as a proposed training programme with an explicit data budget.

#### 8. Computational psychoanalysis: bi-logic, free energy, cybernetics and the limits of formalising Lacan

Matte Blanco's bi-logic is the one psychoanalytic theory with a family of formal reconstructions, none canonical: chaos-theoretic framing, an ultrametric reading in which symmetric logic becomes a hierarchical-clustering space, p-adic tree dynamics, sequent-calculus and quantum-logic treatments, and a group-theoretic book that gave the field the phrase "computational psychoanalysis" (Matte Blanco 1975; Rayner 1995; Lauro-Grotto 2008; Murtagh 2012; Khrennikov 2007; Battilotti 2014; Battilotti n.d.a; Battilotti n.d.b; Battilotti, Borozan and Lauro Grotto 2023; Iurato 2018; Tomic 2020). The only critical survey is a blog essay, which judges the ultrametric line checkable, the sequent calculi checkable as logic, the quantum machinery overloaded with unjustified baggage and the group-theoretic line "undeveloped analogies" (Joncas 2021). No treatment has clinical validation beyond illustrative examples. A "Tandon" reference for computational psychoanalysis could not be located and should not be cited.

The neuropsychoanalytic bridge to the free-energy principle is mathematically rigorous on the free-energy side and a verbal dictionary on the psychoanalytic side: ego as precision-weighted higher-level priors, primary process as high-entropy lower-level activity, repression as explaining-away, and, in Solms' version, consciousness as felt uncertainty around homeostatic settling points (Carhart-Harris and Friston 2010; Carhart-Harris and Friston 2019; Solms 2019; Solms and Friston 2018; Solms 2021; Hopkins 2016; Connolly 2018; Holmes and Nolte 2019; Rabeyron and Massicotte 2020). None of these papers models a Lacanian construct; they are Freudian, and they are the mandatory predecessor citations for any "psychoanalysis and free energy" language in the series. Lacan's own information-theoretic engagement is historically well established as a response to Shannon, Wiener and the Macy conferences, and the alpha-beta-gamma-delta automaton of Seminar II is the one genuinely checkable finite-state fragment in his work (Lacan 1954-55/1988; Lacan 1966/2006; Fink 1996; Liu 2010a; Liu 2010b; Johnston 2008; Guido 2024).

The direct computational modelling of Lacanian constructs consists of the Gadalla group's annotation study, which now includes the capitalist discourse as a fifth label and reports Krippendorff's alpha of 0.70 for Analyst, 0.60 for University, 0.55 for Master and Capitalist and 0.52 for Hysteric across three raters, with no held-out prediction, classifier or external criterion (Gadalla, Nikoletseas and Amazonas 2026). No graph-neural-network, agent-based or reinforcement-learning simulation of the four discourses or the signifying chain was found, so that appears open, although an eight-query search cannot prove it. Humanities uses of Lacan as a lens on AI are non-computational (Possati 2020; Possati 2021; Millar 2021; Magee, Arora and Munn 2023). On topology, the Borromean property, non-orientable surfaces and fundamental groups are correct mathematics in Vappereau and the edited collections, while statements of the form "the subject is a cross-cap" are not propositions in mathematics and the graph of desire is a diagram, not a graph with edge semantics (Greenshields 2017; Ragland and Milovanovic 2004; Burgoyne 2000; Vappereau 1985-1997). The critical consensus is that the mathemes are ideograms with a fixed reading grammar rather than terms of a calculus, that Lacan's formulas of sexuation are deliberately ill-formed, and that any model assigning numbers to S1, S2, $ and a is a new construction owned by its author (Fink 2004; Milner 1995; Badiou 2006; Hallward and Peden 2012).

The validated toolbox that engineers actually reach for is network psychometrics, dynamical-systems clinical psychology and stochastic cusp fitting, each with published estimation and reliability procedures (Borsboom 2017; Hayes et al. 2007; Hayes and Andrews 2020; van der Maas, Kolstein and van der Pligt 2003; Cobb and Watson 1980; Guastello, Koopmans and Pincus 2009). The DISC-by-OCEAN "psychometric tensor" has no construct-validity basis; the nearest validated relatives are multi-trait multi-method matrices, the interpersonal circumplex and hierarchical Big Two models, and the treatise should describe the tensor as an engineering feature space.

#### 9. EU and Dutch law, occupational-safety guidance and prior sonification of human state

Article 5(1)(f) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 prohibits the placing on the market, putting into service or use of AI systems to infer emotions of a natural person in the areas of workplace and education, except for medical or safety reasons; Article 3(39) defines an emotion recognition system as inferring emotions or intentions from biometric data, Article 3(34) defines biometric data without the GDPR's unique-identification qualifier, and Article 113 makes Chapter II applicable from 2 February 2025 (European Parliament and Council 2024). Recital 44 states the legislator's reason, the limited reliability, lack of specificity and limited generalisability of emotion inference, and the Commission's guidelines footnote Barrett and colleagues' review as the scientific basis, so the series can present its refusal to infer emotion as both scientifically and legally principled (European Parliament and Council 2024; Barrett et al. 2019). The adopted guidelines on prohibited practices read "emotions" broadly and non-exhaustively, forbid circumvention by calling them attitudes, list eye tracking, heartbeats, EEG, ECG, keystroke dynamics and voice as biometric inputs, treat "emotional arousal and anxiousness" as emotions, treat a control room as a workplace, and give as prohibited examples both call-centre voice emotion tracking and monitoring the "emotional tone" of work teams; averaging over a crew does not help because each operator's emotion is inferred first (European Commission 2025; Patel 2025). The medical or safety exception is narrow, general stress monitoring and burnout detection at work remain prohibited, and even where a specific danger justifies stress measurement the data may not be reused for performance assessment. Physical states such as fatigue and pain are carved out by Recital 18, which is the one person-state channel with regulatory precedent.

A fixed, human-authored parameter-mapping sonification of process and interaction data is arguably not an AI system at all under the guidelines on the AI-system definition, which exclude systems that follow predefined explicit instructions and systems intended solely for descriptive analysis and visualisation, although this reading has not been tested by any authority (European Commission 2025). If the MPN is an AI system in a control room, Annex III high-risk status is likely under point 2 (safety components in energy, water or gas supply) or point 4(b) (monitoring the performance and behaviour of workers), with worker-information duties under Article 26(7) applying from 2 August 2026, and Article 50(3) transparency duties for any permitted emotion recognition (European Parliament and Council 2024). Under the GDPR, HRV, EDA and EEG about operators are health data by Recital 35 even where they are not biometric in the GDPR sense, the Dutch UAVG Article 30 offers employers no ground for monitoring healthy workers, explicit consent is not free in employment, and the only defensible route is occupational-medicine processing under Article 9(2)(h) by a physician bound by secrecy, which defeats a supervisor-facing display (European Parliament and Council 2016; Staten-Generaal 2018; Article 29 Data Protection Working Party 2017). The Dutch regulator forced two employers to stop processing wearable movement and sleep data in 2016, holding that consent is not free in a financially dependent relationship and that vendor-side or anonymised processing does not cure it, and it is now the coordinating supervisor for the AI Act prohibitions (NJB 2016; CMS 2016; Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens 2025; Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens n.d.). Even a non-biometric, non-AI sonification of operator behaviour is a facility "suitable for" observing behaviour or performance under Works Council Act Article 27(1)(l) and requires prior works-council consent, alongside a DPIA and purpose limitation (Staten-Generaal 1971).

Occupational-safety guidance points the same way: ISO 45003 frames psychosocial risk as a participatory organisational process, EU-OSHA warns of the OSH risks of AI-based worker management and the guidelines note that continuous wearable monitoring may itself increase work stress, and the Framework Directive's hierarchy places fixing the workload above monitoring the worker (ISO 2021; EU-OSHA 2022; EU-OSHA 2023; Council Directive 89/391/EEC; ILO/WHO 2022). Regulatory precedent for a human-state channel exists only for fatigue: the SAFTE model takes sleep, wake and work schedules as inputs and outputs a population-level effectiveness score validated at R squared of 0.884 on 10,659 PVT sessions from 178 cabin crew, and aviation regulators require that such models be advisory, never a go/no-go criterion, because they predict population averages rather than an individual's instantaneous state (Roma et al. 2012; CASA 2014; FRA 2022; ICAO 2020; NRC n.d.).

Prior sonification of human state confirms the same boundary. Sonified EEG is interpretable only after training and only for gross discrete events such as seizures, movement sonification uses simple mappings and common-sense metaphors, and the closest precedent for sonifying team state rendered the positional dynamics of sports players, observable behaviour rather than inferred affect (Loui et al. 2014; Hermann et al. 2006; Hinterberger and Baier 2005; Väljamäe et al. 2013; Vogt et al. 2009; Pauletto and Hunt 2006; Effenberg 2006; Höner, Hermann and Grunow 2004). The Meeting Mediator fed speaking-time balance back to participants themselves, not to a supervisor, and the sociometric-badge programme drew sustained criticism as workplace surveillance (Kim et al. 2008; Pentland 2008). Regulators treat ECG-based emotion recognition as squarely within the prohibition (Hasnul et al. 2021). No operational safety-critical system was found that continuously infers and displays a team's psychological or emotional state to a supervisor; the live analogues are fatigue-effectiveness scores in scheduling tools and workload indices derived from system events, both of which avoid emotion inference by construction. The Lacanian and psychoanalytic vocabulary of the series is legitimate as an interpretive frame for team dynamics, but an engineering chapter must separate that frame from the implemented signal chain and state that no system output identifies a natural person's emotion or intention.

### Open Questions

Page numbers for the Norton, Polity, Cambridge and Seuil editions of Lacan, and for Fink, Evans, Bracher, Copjec, Chiesa, Le Gaufey and the other secondary Lacanian works, were not verified against any publisher page; the only verified page pair is Miller's suture claim at page 44 of the French and page 29 of the English text. Seminar XXII has no official edition and can only be cited to Ornicar? extracts or Gallagher's unofficial translation. The ScienceDirect DOI of Gadalla et al. 2023, the authorship and venue of the ResearchGate item on Lacan's computational model, and the exact wording of the 1955 cybernetics lecture remain unchecked, and whether Vappereau's or Soury's knot-theoretic statements have ever been reviewed by a topologist is unknown.

The contents of IEC 62682:2022 Tables 5 and 7 (pages 83 and 87) were not seen and are not publicly reproduced; the ISA-18.2-2016 table number, wording and figures are known only through the vendor and HSE-adjacent reproductions listed under theme 3, which were obtained through a separate Perplexity cross-check rather than through the nine research files and have not been independently verified against the standard. The ISA-18.2 lifecycle stage list, alarm-flood definition and "time to respond" definition were not verified as clause text; EEMUA 191 edition 4 contents and any changed benchmarks were not fetched; the CSB Texas City findings on fatigue and staffing, the Three Mile Island alarm counts and the NUREG revision numbers were not confirmed.

Effect sizes and coefficients were deliberately omitted wherever they were not seen: Watson and Sanderson 2004 task-sharing performance, the Queensland enhanced-oximetry percent-correct figures, Momtahan et al. 1993 identification percentages, ISO 7731 numeric audibility requirements, IEC 60601-1-8 Amendment 2 details, the correlation coefficients of Lerdahl and Krumhansl 2007 and Farbood 2012, the decoding-accuracy percentages of Juslin and Laukka 2003, NEO-PI-R and BFI reliability coefficients, the speech-stress review's accuracy ranges, and the cohort details of van de Leemput et al. 2014, whose fetch was blocked. Several DOIs (Ratcliff and McKoon 2008, Scheffer 2009, Strogatz 2000, Yerkes and Dodson 1908, Carhart-Harris and Friston 2010, the REBUS paper, Hopkins 2016) are from memory and need CrossRef resolution, and the Music Perception DOIs are pattern-inferred.

Three absences are findings in themselves but were established by eight-query searches and not by exhaustive review: there is no controlled field or simulator study of continuous sonification of plant or team state in an industrial control room; there is no published fit of a Kramers escape rate, or of any Arrhenius-type rate, to human operator fatigue, workload collapse or attentional lapses; and there is no operational safety-critical system that displays a live team psychological state to a supervisor. Related open items are whether anyone has tested reader comprehension of staff notation as a representation of non-musical state, whether any generator has been conditioned on more than four affect dimensions, whether any peer-reviewed DISC factor-analytic study exists outside vendor reports, and how individual physiology could be aggregated to a team state at all.

On the legal side, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens' February 2025 summary of its emotion-recognition consultation and its employee-monitoring conditions page returned HTTP 403 and their exact positions are unread; ISO 45003's clause text is paywalled; no EDPB opinion specifically on emotion recognition in employment was located; the fine levels under Article 99 were not re-read; and whether a fixed-rule sonification of interaction logs escapes the AI-system definition is an arguable position untested by any authority. Finally, the Valyu search channel was not used in this programme because no API key was available, so all coverage rests on general web search and direct fetches within a budget of eight searches and six fetches per question.
