[[OT Segment 1]]
[[datacenter]]

Networking systems provide the high-speed, low-latency fabric necessary to tie hyperscale clusters together.

1. **Network Devices:** Switches, routers, industrial firewalls, and network controllers.
2. **Network Interface Cards (NICs):** Hardware that connects servers to the broader network.
3. **Optical Communication:** High-speed optical circuit switching and optical network infrastructure.
4. **Hardware Transport Fabrics:** Ethernet infrastructures and advanced low-latency hardware transports (like Google's Falcon) tailored for hyperscaler environments.
5. **Networking Software/OS:** Open network operating systems and interfaces such as ONIE, SAI, and SONiC.
