[[datacenter]]

These systems encompass the industrial automation and physical infrastructure required to keep the data center running safely and efficiently. Because they manage physical processes, these are typically the systems governed by operational security standards like IEC 62443.

1. **Power Distribution:** Systems that handle the delivery and regulation of electricity throughout the facility. This includes backup and emergency power supplies.
2. **Cooling Environments:** Critical for maintaining safe operating temperatures for high-density computational infrastructure. Advanced hyperscale cooling components include:
    1. Immersion cooling and Cold Plate systems.
    2. Door Heat Exchangers and Coolant Distribution Units.
    3. Heat Reuse systems and Technology Cooling System Pipe Distribution.
3. **Building Management Systems (BMS):** The overarching control systems that monitor and manage the facility's mechanical and electrical equipment.
4. **Fire Suppression and Detection:** Automated systems designed to detect and extinguish fires to protect critical assets and human safety.
5. **Physical Security Systems:** Systems designed to control physical access and monitor the premises (e.g., networked video surveillance cameras and recorders).
6. **Rack & Power Structures:** The physical racks and modular data center enclosures that house the IT equipment.
