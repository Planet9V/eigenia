[[datacenter]]

The bedrock of hyperscale infrastructure relies on the lowest-level software (firmware) and dedicated management modules to ensure system integrity.

- **Baseboard Management Controllers (BMC) & Hardware Management Modules:** Specialized microcontrollers used for remote monitoring and out-of-band management of servers.
- **Trusted Platform Modules (TPM) & Silicon Roots of Trust (RoT):** Hardware components used for secure boot, cryptographic key management, and device attestation.
- **Firmware and Microcode:** Updatable, foundational software residing internally on chips or on external flash memory that controls devices like CPUs, GPUs, and network cards.
- **Data Center Telemetry (DCT):** Systems that collect performance and health data across the data center infrastructure.
