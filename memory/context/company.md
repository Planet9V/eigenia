# Eigenia B.V. and Eigenia Labs
- Dutch private limited company (B.V.), Amsterdam; think tank for applied complexity science and cyber-physical risk; Eigenia Labs is the open-research arm.
- Mission: safeguard clean water, healthy food and sustainable energy by replacing compliance checklists with reproducible mathematical models, open standards and catastrophe-calibrated risk models.
- Five stated rules: safety first; people make the difference; fact-based decisions; right results, right way (the source lists four, numbered 1, 2, 4, 5).
- Domains: eigenia.nl (live, Railway); eigenia.com (named, not wired). Repo: github.com/Planet9V/eigenia.
- Launch posts (EN/NL) exist in LAUNCH_POST.md; brand assets in assets/.
