## Additional Standards and Mappings

The following table maps IEC 62443 zones and conduits to typical datacenter OT subsystems. This mapping is used throughout the CyHAZOPs analysis in Chapters 4–12.

| IEC 62443 Zone/Conduit | Datacenter OT Subsystem | Example Components | Security Level Target (SL-T) |
|------------------------|------------------------|-------------------|-----------------------------|
| Zone 0 – Process       | Cooling loops, power distribution (UPS, PDU) | Chiller controllers, VFDs, static transfer switches | SL 2 (IEC 62443-3-3) |
| Zone 1 – Control       | Building Management System (BMS), Electrical Power Monitoring System (EPMS) | PLCs, RTUs, BMS controllers | SL 3 |
| Zone 2 – Supervisory   | Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM), SCADA servers | DCIM servers, historian, operator workstations | SL 3 |
| Zone 3 – Operations Management | IT/OT integration gateways, patch management servers | Jump servers, ADMS, SIEM collectors | SL 2 |
| Conduit C1 – OT Network | Control network between Zones 0–2 | Managed switches, firewalls, IDS/IPS | SL 3 (conduit) |
| Conduit C2 – IT/OT Boundary | DMZ between enterprise IT and OT | Next-gen firewall, proxy, VPN concentrator | SL 4 (conduit) |

*Table 1: IEC 62443 zone/conduit mapping for hyperscale datacenter OT systems. Security level targets are derived from risk assessment per IEC 62443-3-2 [IEC, 2020].*
