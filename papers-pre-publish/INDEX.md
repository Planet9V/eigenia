# papers-pre-publish: index

Two organised bodies of work sit here, each with its own index.

## MPN

The McKenney-Lacan Musical Psychometric Notation programme. Theory, mathematics, the bias and personality corpora, the notation and engine, seven scored plays, and the paper series.

Start at `MPN/00_INDEX/INDEX.md` for the table of contents, `MPN/00_INDEX/SUBJECT-INDEX.md` to find a subject, `MPN/00_INDEX/FILE-INDEX.md` to find a file.

304 files in the working tree. The December 2025 archive is kept whole in `MPN/09_SNAPSHOTS`. `MPN/_attic` holds superseded duplicates only and is safe to delete.

## OT

IEC 62443 and TS 50701 engineering material: standards references, requirement sets, workbooks, the CRL engagement, platform PRDs, the Babylon 3D visualisation work with its asset pack, and two site data documents.

Start at `OT/00_INDEX/README.md`. 116 files, hash-verified against the archive copy it was taken from.

## Everything else

The loose files and folders at this level are earlier working material and are not indexed: the TACAM papers, the hyperscale architecture work, the DEXPI and CycloneDX folder, the research equations, the WP thesis series and the proposals backlog.
