==============================================================================
PART 1. THE ROUNDING RULE
==============================================================================
  simplex grid: 20,301 points at a step of 1/200
  live pitch table, trauma at or below 0.6 so the modes are
  Real Dorian, Symbolic Lydian, Imaginary Phrygian

  delta | in margin | rounding moves | rounded differs | mean |err| | max |err|
        |           | a degree       | from argmax     | in cents  | in cents
  ------|-----------|----------------|-----------------|-----------|----------
   0.02 |    3.44%  |    100.00%     |      14.74%     |    34.69  |   50.00
   0.05 |    9.21%  |    100.00%     |       6.96%     |    32.39  |   50.00
   0.10 |   18.41%  |    100.00%     |       5.72%     |    31.79  |   50.00
   0.15 |   27.13%  |    100.00%     |       6.39%     |    31.72  |   50.00
   0.20 |   35.36%  |    100.00%     |       8.62%     |    31.80  |   50.00
   0.30 |   50.33%  |    100.00%     |      11.76%     |    32.11  |   50.00

  THE COLUMN THAT DECIDES IT is the fourth. It is the share of points
  inside the margin where the ROUNDED blend is a different scale from
  the one argmax would have chosen. Where it is zero, rounding has
  snapped every blended degree back onto the winning mode and the
  interpolation has bought nothing that reaches a notated pitch.

  Multiply the first column by the fourth and the finding is plain:
    delta 0.05: rounding leaves a scale different from argmax on 0.64% of the whole simplex
    delta 0.20: rounding leaves a scale different from argmax on 3.05% of the whole simplex
  So rounding inside Phi snaps the blend back onto the winning mode
  almost everywhere. The interpolation S3 chose over hysteresis and a
  dead band survives into a notated pitch on under one per cent of
  states at delta = 0.05. Rounding does not degrade the interpolation.
  It very nearly deletes it.

  AND THE TIE IS EXACTLY WHERE THE ROUNDING CONVENTION DECIDES THE
  SCALE. At a two-way tie the weights are 0.5 and 0.5, so every degree
  on which the two modes differ by one semitone lands on a half. Take
  the Real and Symbolic tie on the live table, Dorian against Lydian:
    Dorian            [0, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 10]
    Lydian            [0, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11]
    blend at the tie  [0.0, 2.0, 3.5, 5.5, 7.0, 9.0, 10.5]
    round half up     [0, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11]  -> Lydian
    bankers rounding  [0, 2, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10]  -> not one of the seven
  One convention returns one of the two inputs; the other returns a
  scale that is neither. The round built in to Python is the second.
  This is not a detail to leave to a language. Whichever rule is
  adopted decides the scale at precisely the states interpolation was
  chosen for, and it has to be written down.

  cross-check against S2's closed form for the margin area, 2d - d^2:
    delta 0.05: measured 0.0921, closed form 0.0975, difference 0.0054
    delta 0.20: measured 0.3536, closed form 0.3600, difference 0.0064
  The grid and the closed form agree, so the margin is being computed
  the way S2 and S3 describe it and the rest of the table can be read.

  THE RECOMMENDATION FOLLOWS FROM THE TABLE AND FROM ONE PRECEDENT.
  S3 section 2.1 already settles the same question for dynamics: "The
  output of Phi is the marking, not the velocity: the velocity interval
  is the pre-image." Do the same here. Phi emits the blended degree as a
  continuous value in cents; the NOTATED pitch is its rounded pre-image,
  a rendering property of the notation path and not a property of the
  mapping. Then:
    - the jump set of the mode parameter stays the single surface
      tau = 0.6 and nothing on the simplex, which is what S3 proves and
      what rounding inside Phi would destroy;
    - the audio path carries the exact value through pitch bend, so the
      delta experiment runs on a synthesiser that takes cents and needs
      no microtonal notation renderer, which S3 says does not exist;
    - the rounding rule stops being a blocking number for the THEORY and
      becomes a setting on one renderer;
    - and the cost is bounded and stated: notation and audio disagree by
      at most a quarter tone, only inside the margin, on the share of
      the simplex the first column gives.

==============================================================================
PART 2. K_MAX, AND WHETHER THE HARMONIC PARAMETER CAN REJOIN A11
==============================================================================
  generators are involutions: True; fixed-point free: True
  action transitive: True; Cayley diameter: 5
  distance distribution over all 576 ordered pairs: [24, 72, 144, 192, 120, 24]
  S3 section 2.5 gives 24, 72, 144, 192, 120, 24. Reproduced above, so
  the graph this script reasons on is the graph S3 reasons on.
  the LR alternation visits all 24 triads exactly once and closes: True

  S3 records that the harmonic parameter is a function on P x P and on
  the chord already sounding, which is exactly the dependency it uses to
  rule out hysteresis and a dead band for the mode selector, and that it
  has the first-frame problem too. It then asks, in one sentence and
  without computing it, whether the parameter can be re-expressed as a
  function of the current state alone. It can, and the computation says
  at what value of k_max.

  ABSOLUTE form: position = round(k_max * f(state)) around the LR cycle,
  from a fixed origin. A function on P alone. No previous chord, no
  previous state, no first frame problem, A11 intact.
  RELATIVE form: the shipped proposal. A change of size d moves the
  chord round(k_max * d) positions from wherever it is.

  The two agree on the SIZE of every move by construction. Where they
  part company is REACH, and reach is computable:

   k_max | chords the absolute form can ever visit | of 24 | full cycle
  -------|-----------------------------------------|-------|-----------
       1 |                   2                     |  8.3% | no
       4 |                   5                     | 20.8% | no
       5 |                   6                     | 25.0% | no
      10 |                  11                     | 45.8% | no
      11 |                  12                     | 50.0% | no
      12 |                  13                     | 54.2% | no
      13 |                  14                     | 58.3% | no
      16 |                  17                     | 70.8% | no
      20 |                  21                     | 87.5% | no
      22 |                  23                     | 95.8% | no
      23 |                  24                     | 100.0% | yes
      24 |                  24                     | 100.0% | yes

  The relative form reaches all 24 by accumulating moves whatever k_max
  is. The absolute form reaches min(k_max + 1, 24). So the two
  formulations have the same codomain at exactly one value, k_max = 23,
  and at 24 and above the absolute form wraps, which is the artefact S3
  already rules out.

  And the value has to clear S3's own constraint, which is about the
  metric rather than the cycle: how far along the Cayley graph a full
  trauma sweep can carry a character, from every starting triad.

   k_max | greatest Cayley distance a full sweep reaches | from how many
         | (worst starting triad)                       | triads is the
         |                                              | antipode reached
  -------|----------------------------------------------|---------------
       5 |                     4                        |       0 of 24
      10 |                     4                        |       0 of 24
      11 |                     4                        |      12 of 24
      12 |                     4                        |      12 of 24
      13 |                     5                        |      24 of 24
      16 |                     5                        |      24 of 24
      20 |                     5                        |      24 of 24
      23 |                     5                        |      24 of 24

  Reproduces S3 section 2.5: nothing below 11 reaches the diameter from
  any triad, nothing below 13 reaches it from every triad, and the
  defensible range is 13 to 23.

  THE RECOMMENDATION. k_max = 23, and with it the absolute form.
  Three independent reasons, none of them a taste judgement:
    1. It is the only value at which the state-only formulation has the
       same reachable set as the change-based one, so adopting it is
       what lets the harmonic parameter be re-expressed as a function of
       the current state. That restores A11 decomposability for the one
       parameter of the six that lacks it, and removes the first-frame
       problem, which in therapy is the opening of every session.
    2. It is at the top of S3's own defensible range, so a destroyed
       character can arrive at the far side of the harmonic space from
       every starting triad rather than from half of them.
    3. It is the largest value that stays injective. At 24 the map wraps
       by an artefact of the modulus.

  WHAT IT DOES NOT FIX, and the estimate is worth nothing if this is
  not said with it. S3 finds that chain position and Cayley distance do
  not rise together, so a larger change in trauma can produce a smaller
  harmonic move. k_max = 23 does not repair that and makes the worst
  case plainest: at a sweep of 23 positions the character ends ONE
  generator from where they started.

  Read that column downward. It is not monotone and it is not close to
  monotone. Choosing k_max does not decide whether the harmonic move
  carries a magnitude or an index, which is S3's open repair and stays
  the author's. What k_max = 23 does is make the parameter a function of
  the state, which is the thing A11 needs and the thing no other value
  delivers.
