# MPN music and score series: plan of record

Written 2026-09-12. Supersedes `08_PAPERS/SERIES-PLAN-FOR-REVIEW.md`, which was drafted before the archive was opened and before the assertions were reviewed.

## Where the programme stands

The material is organised and indexed: 326 files in the working tree, every path in the indexes verified. The theory, the primers, the bias atlas, the notation, the engine and seven scored plays are all in hand and readable.

The theory has been reviewed. Eleven assertions went through a Skeptic, a Constraint Guardian and a User Advocate, then arbitration. Nine survive, two are withdrawn, one is amended on evidence, one needs a decision from Jim. Two assertions have been tested on 31,078 scored beats: trauma and entropy are independent, confirming A5; the fragmentation and orchestration scalars correlate at 0.919, refuting A8.

S1 exists but predates all of that. S2, S3 and S4 have never been written.

## What gets written

Four papers, in order, each built on Jim's own documents and each reviewed before the next begins.

**S1, the theory, revised.** Mostly subtraction. Withdraw A9, objet a as a divergence quantity, because the system maintains no internal model to diverge from. Withdraw A10, the audience as a term, because it has no implementation and its arithmetic breaks the unit bound. Amend A8 on the evidence now in hand. Fold in the Cognitive Bias Atlas as the bias layer's foundation: four domains, thirty nodes, CB-001 to CB-030. Rebuild the dialogue and bias material on `01_THEORY/01_core/08_POLYPHONY_AND_DISSONANCE_IN_DIALOGUE.md`, `09_ADVERSARIAL_COUNTERPOINT_ATTACK_DEFEND.md` and `03_TOPOLOGY_OF_DIAD_AND_TRIAD.md` rather than on my reconstruction of them.

**S2, the mathematics.** Built on the ten primers in `01_THEORY/02_primers`, with the dynamics from the core folder: Ising, Granovetter, bifurcation, critical slowing. States each derivation and cites each borrowed result to its source.

**S3, the mapping.** Phi from psychological state to musical parameter, built on `04_NOTATION_ENGINE` and the thirty bias-to-music mappings. Every row carries its evidence tier. Fixes the four incompatible register-to-mode tables once Jim has chosen one.

**S4, the application.** The Conductor and the corpus: text in, state derived, score generated. Built on `05_DATA`, the seven scored plays with their source texts and generator, and on the audit already done of the implementation.

## How each is reviewed

The same gate that produced the Block A arbitration, because it worked. Skeptic first, then Constraint Guardian and User Advocate together, then arbitration with a decision log. No paper proceeds to the next while an objection is unresolved.

## Decisions owed by Jim

**The register-to-mode table.** Four incompatible versions exist and no paper can defend any of them until one is chosen. This blocks S3. It is an afternoon at a piano rather than an analysis.

**A8.** Either give fragmentation and orchestration genuinely different functional forms, so that one is not a reweighting of the other, or admit one intensity variable and simplify. This blocks the S1 revision.

The bias count is no longer open; the Atlas settles it at thirty in four domains.
