# Invitations, one per profession

Three ready-to-send messages, one for each of the three professions the pack is
built for. Fill in the name at the top and the link at the bottom. Nothing else
needs changing. Send one message, one link, and nothing attached.

The three paths are three professions and not three seats. Send the composer
message to every composer or music director you want to ask, the therapist
message to every music therapist, and the researcher message to every
researcher or methodologist. There is no cap on how many people answer on a
path, no target for how the answers split between the three, and no requirement
that the three come out anywhere near level.

---

## 1. Composer or music director

**Subject:** A listening pack, about 30 minutes

[Name],

I am writing a set of papers on choosing music from a description of a dramatic
situation, and four of the claims in them are settled by ear rather than by
argument. I have put all four into one page you work through in a browser.

What it is: seven scales on one melody, seven items to judge as a scale or as
mistuning, and ten pairs of chords to rate for distance. About 30 minutes.
Headphones, or speakers you trust.

Every sound in it was synthesised by a script. Nothing is a recording of a
person, a session or a performance, and nobody is measured. You are being asked
as a professional, for a judgement, and the judgement is the data. Nothing
leaves your browser while you work. At the end you download one text file and
send it back to me however you like.

You choose whether to be named, acknowledged without the judgement attached to
your name, or kept anonymous, and I will send you the results either way.

Why I am asking you: deciding which scale carries which dramatic situation is a
choice you make as a matter of craft, and the material I have disagrees with
itself about that choice in four different ways.

The same page is going to other composers and music directors, and to music
therapists and researchers on their own paths. Answers are read together rather
than one at a time, and they come back over weeks rather than in a batch.

## 2. Music therapist

**Subject:** A listening pack and one design question, about 25 minutes

[Name],

I am writing a set of papers on rendering a rating as music, and part of it is a
tool for therapists. Two of its claims are settled by ear rather than by
argument, and one design decision needs a practitioner rather than a desk. I
have put all three into one page you work through in a browser.

What it is: seven scales on one melody, eight pairs of tones to judge as the

profile, and the decision most likely to be wrong is what its balanced middle
should sound like, which is a question about what you would actually do at the
end of a session rather than a question about sound.

The same page is going to other music therapists, and to composers and

## 3. Researcher or methodologist

**Subject:** A listening pack and its method, about 25 minutes

[Name],

I am writing a set of papers in which four claims turn on discrimination
judgements, and I have built the stimuli and put them in one page you work
through in a browser. The last part of it asks you to take the design apart.

What it is: seven items to judge as a scale or as mistuning, ten pairs of chords
to rate for distance, eight pairs of tones to judge as same or different, and
then the four claims set out with what would falsify each. About 25 minutes.
Headphones, or speakers you trust.

Every sound in it was synthesised by a script. Nothing is a recording of a
person, a session or a performance, and nobody is measured. You are being asked
as a professional, for a judgement, and the judgement is the data. Nothing
leaves your browser while you work. At the end you download one text file and
send it back to me however you like.

You choose whether to be named, acknowledged without the judgement attached to
your name, or kept anonymous, and I will send you the results either way.

Why I am asking you: this is an expert panel rather than a sample, with no
pre-registration, no counterbalancing and no power calculation, and rather than
guess what it would take to make a result from it citable I would rather you
told me, including what about the stimulus construction would invalidate a
result before the statistics start.

The same page is going to other researchers and methodologists, and to
composers and music therapists on their own paths. How many answer on each path
is whatever it turns out to be, the split between the three is not balanced by
design, and answers come back over weeks rather than in a batch. Part of what I
am asking you is what that does to what the returns can carry.
