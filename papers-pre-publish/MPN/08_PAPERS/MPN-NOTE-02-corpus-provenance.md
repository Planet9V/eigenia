## The finding

The plan for this paper named its sources: ten mathematical primers and a set of core theory chapters, from which the formal apparatus was to be assembled. A source pass over that material, read end to end rather than through its indexes, produced a result that changes what this paper can be.

The ten primers are competent summaries of standard mathematics; seven close with an application section and three do not. None contains the nine-component state, the registers as magnitudes, the transformation, or any musical object whatever. Every one carries an author field of the form "AEON Research Division (Agent 1 - The Tensor)", naming an agent or a swarm, and several carry classification markings.

The core chapters are in the same position. Twenty-one of the twenty-two are signed "AEON Research Division", by named swarms such as "Swarm 2 - The Physicists" and "Swarm 5 - The Critics"; the twenty-second is signed "System Architecture Designer". They develop the vocabulary the theory uses and do it with real mathematics correctly stated: the transverse-field Ising Hamiltonian, the Langevin equation, Granovetter's threshold rule, the early-warning indicators. In not one is any component of the nine-component state mapped into any variable of any of those models.

The larger unified-theory directory is a third body of work: sixty-one files, forty-four of them carrying a McKenney author field, built on a six-component vector that is not the nine-component state and whose components do not correspond to it. It carries 147 display equations. What can be said is that the directory's own critique documents are the strongest material in it, and that at least one stated axiom is false: `01_Foundations.md:195` asserts that collective entropy exceeds the sum of individual entropies and calls it synergy, which is the negation of subadditivity and holds only when the parts are independent, in which case the quantity is exactly zero.

What becomes of that material is a separate question from what this paper can build on, and it should not be left implied. The primers and the core chapters are competent statements of standard mathematics and remain useful as vocabulary; they are not cited as results by this series and should not be cited as results anywhere, because none contains a component of the state. The unified volumes stand as a distinct body of work on a distinct vector and are not superseded by this series; they are simply not its foundation. One item is not a matter of standing but of fact: the synergy axiom is false as written and should be corrected or struck wherever it is relied on.

So the honest answer to what formal apparatus this theory has is: less than the plan assumed, and not where the plan looked. What it has is the state definition and the simplex constraint from S1, the scalarisation algebra the A8 amendment turns on, the neo-Riemannian group as a codomain for the harmonic operator, which the sister series has already set out [2], and a collection of named dynamical models correctly stated somewhere and attached to nothing. Decision 2 of the log of 12 September [18] asks that the dynamics apparatus be developed as apparatus even where only partly useful, and section 7 does that.
