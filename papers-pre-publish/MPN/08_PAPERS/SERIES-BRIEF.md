## Non-negotiable positions of the series (decided with the author 2026-09-11)

1. **Lawful-by-design.** EU AI Act Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Art. 5(1)(f) prohibits AI systems that infer emotions of natural persons in the workplace (applicable since 2 February 2025); the Commission's guidelines C(2025) 5052 read "emotions" broadly (stress-based anxiety, emotional arousal, burnout, team "emotional tone"); GDPR Art. 9 treats physiological data as health data; the Dutch Works Councils Act (WOR) Art. 27(1)(k)/(l) requires works-council consent for any personnel-monitoring facility. Therefore: MPN sonifies **plant state, task load, interaction tempo and adversary discourse**, never an inferred emotion, mood, stress or arousal of an operator; **no biometric input about staff** (voice, face, HRV, EDA, EEG, gaze, keystroke) in the deployed signal chain; person-level psychological modelling is confined to (a) adversary/threat-actor modelling (the original "Seldon" use: threat actors are not employees), (b) simulation, training exercises and the crisis game, (c) an advisory schedule-based fatigue channel only, following the SAFTE-FAST precedent. Every paper states this once, in its own words, and Paper 4 makes it a set of design invariants.
2. **Honesty about the codebase.** The MPN Conductor is a rule-based sonification prototype with an unvalidated proposed input space and untrained ML scaffolding. No paper may describe PSYCHOSCORE as a trained model, the 57-D vector as an input to generation, voice/personality extraction as working, or any early-warning lead time as measured. The repo's own reviewer file says "Zero empirical validation".
3. **Honesty about Lacan.** The four discourses are a finite combinatorial object (four terms, four slots, Z4 quarter-turn) stated by Lacan himself; the Borromean property is real knot theory used as a structural constraint; mathemes are ideograms, not variables with values; the numerical semantics are the working group's own construction and are labelled so. Do not claim Lacan's topology is mathematically sound; cite Sokal & Bricmont and the best defences (Plotnitsky 2002; Fink 2004) once.
4. **Honesty about psychometrics.** Big Five is the replicated trait taxonomy; DISC is a lay vocabulary without independent validity evidence; Dark Tetrad (SD4) is a research instrument, never an operational flag; DISC⊗OCEAN has no psychometric precedent and is an engineering feature space; physiology indexes arousal and load, not emotion (Barrett et al. 2019).
5. **Honesty about dynamics.** The stochastic cusp (Cobb; Grasman, van der Maas & Wagenmakers 2009) and Kramers escape (Kramers 1940; Hänggi, Talkner & Borkovec 1990) are the same overdamped SDE; nobody has measured Kramers parameters for a human operator; say "if operator state obeys ..., then ...". Do not call the cusp potential "free energy" without distinguishing it from variational free energy (Friston). Cite predecessors: Carhart-Harris & Friston 2010; Solms 2019; Gadalla, Nikoletseas & Amazonas 2026; Iurato 2018; Borsboom 2017; Scheffer et al. 2009; van de Leemput et al. 2014.
6. **Evidence tiers for musical mappings** (from q5): tempo→arousal STRONG; mode→valence CONVENTIONAL (Western, tempo-dominated); dissonance/roughness→tension COMPONENT; harmonic distance→instability SUPPORTED within tonal idiom; PLR group = D24 is MATHEMATICS not psychology; metre irregularity→uncertainty THEORY; timbre→arousal MODERATE; perceived vs induced emotion must be kept separate. Every mapping in a table carries its tier.
7. **OXOT is never named.** Eigenia specifies the method; the implementation is not named. Cite the GitHub repository as the reference implementation only in Paper 3 and Paper 4 by URL.
