The five statuses are used as in the S2 and S3 ledgers: **VERIFIED** for a source opened from this session that says what the paper says it says, **VERIFIED-NARROWED** for one that supports part of the claim with the unsupported part named, **UNVERIFIED-OFFLINE** for a published source that could not be opened from here, **BROKEN** for a path, line or URL that does not resolve, and **SELF** for a document in this series, with its revision named and its currency stated.

S4 is the only paper in the series with no published literature in its reference list, which is why it carries no UNVERIFIED-OFFLINE row. Everything it cites is either a document in the series, a script, an analysis file in the working tree, or the reference implementation. That makes it the easiest of the five to check and the one where a broken path would be least excusable, since nothing here is beyond reach.

## Claims carried without a citation, on purpose

Sections 3.1 to 3.5 are readings of the score files themselves and are cited to the files rather than to any authority, which is correct: the finding that three of the seven are anthologies is the printed contents block of those files, and a reader disagrees with the file. Section 6.1's three kinds of not-working, stubbed, dead and decorative, is a distinction the paper draws and does not attribute, and section 7.1's account of what determinism does not buy is an argument rather than a report. None of these needs a source and none is given one.

## Not cited, though available

`ARBITRATION-S4.md`, which records the four-reviewer gate this revision passed and its six conditions, is cited by the PRD at its [4] and by the design document at its [4] but not by the paper itself, which names it only in its status line. `REVIEW-S4-skeptic.md`, which produced the seven blocking findings that forced revision 2, is likewise uncited. Neither omission costs the paper anything, since a paper is not obliged to cite its own gate, but a reader tracing how section 3 came to exist has to find those two files without help from the reference list.
