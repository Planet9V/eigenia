## D-2026-09-14-A. BL-8, the bias layer's channel

**Taken: option (e).** The bias layer occupies no musical channel. It is a Layer 3 detector whose output is a marked textual proposal beside the Layer 0 turn that prompted it, which satisfies all three rules of section 4.4 and needs no channel at all.

**Not permanent.** The permanence clause of option (e) is replaced by a stated reopening condition: a detection rate from BL-5 and BL-6. If the detector is shown to find planted moves at a usable rate, the question reopens with evidence it has never had.

**If it reopens, the fourth stave and not articulation.** Option (a) passes all five tests, is the only candidate that does, and stays available. Articulation and voicing is refused now because it is the option a later revision of S3 is most likely to reclaim for a parameter of its own [3].

**What this costs, recorded rather than softened.** It defers for a second time a capability the author asked for twice. It removes half of section 6.2's framing sentence. Assertion B1, that the bias layer is not predicted by the state, stays untested, because nothing renders.

## D-2026-09-14-B. MPN-NOTE-05, both recommendations

**Taken: the rounding pre-image rule.** $\Phi$ does not round. It emits the blended scale degree as a continuous value in cents, and the notated pitch is its rounded pre-image, a property of the notation renderer and not of the mapping. This follows the precedent S3 section 2.1 already sets for dynamics, where the output of $\Phi$ is the marking and the velocity interval is the pre-image [2].

**Taken: $k_{\max} = 23$, with the absolute form.** The harmonic parameter is re-expressed as a function of the current state alone, chord position equal to $\operatorname{round}(23 \cdot f(\text{state}))$ around the $LR$ cycle from a fixed origin, rather than as a move of size $\operatorname{round}(k_{\max}\delta)$ from the chord already sounding. Twenty-three is the only value at which the two formulations have the same reachable set [2].

**What each buys.** The pre-image rule keeps the jump set of the mode parameter at the single surface $\tau = 0.6$, makes the $\delta$ experiment runnable on a synthesiser rather than waiting on a microtonal notation renderer, and stops the rounding rule gating the theory. The absolute form restores A11's decomposability for the one parameter of six that lacked it and removes the first-frame problem, which in a therapy session is the opening of every session.

**What neither fixes, recorded with them.** Chain position and Cayley distance still do not rise together, so a larger change in trauma can produce a smaller harmonic move; whether the harmonic move carries a magnitude or an index remains open and Part C of the listening pack is the test. And under the pre-image rule $\Phi$ ceases to discretise mode at all, so no perturbation budget can be written in that channel, which the arbitration records as finding G2 [1].
