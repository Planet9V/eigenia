- The Staatscourant number 64418 for the AP's DPIA list is taken from a search-listing title; the wetten.overheid.nl consolidated text confirmed everything else about the decision.
- The insider exclusion (D-5) is now anchored in MPN-2's N-27 and MPN-3's E-4 rather than in the writer's inference alone.
- The description of the fatigue annotation's inputs (hours into shift, position in the night, run of consecutive shifts implied by the roster) is the working group's own specification of "schedule-predicted sleep opportunity"; no ledger line describes the SAFTE sleep-prediction algorithm and none is claimed.
- The 12.0 s and 65.0+ s figures from the 20-second-deficit paper are described as "modelled figures"; that characterisation is the series' reading under MPN-1 F-6.
