## 2. Ruling A. The Autonomy mapping is specifiable in parts, and the parts are four

The question put is whether the instrument is specifiable in parts and which parts, given that the Skeptic finds the gap wider than the five unfixed triples and the Guardian finds three of five gradients buildable today.

Both are right about their own half and each is wrong about one step of the other's. Taking the Skeptic first, every item he names is confirmed. Section 5.4's mapping table has five rows and arousal and valence appear in none of them; they appear in section 5.2 as two of the five ratings, they consume two of the five taps section 6.4 budgets, gate G0 requires them anchored, and they reach no state coordinate, no musical parameter and no rule anywhere in the document. Section 7.4's fixture names relational stance, musical variability and session intensity and is silent about them, which is the same fact seen from the output side. Session intensity is said to replace the computed ramp entirely, which states what it replaces and not what it becomes, and no sentence anywhere carries a five-point ordinal into trauma in the unit interval, which is what the eight-marking discretiser of S3 section 2.1 has to read [9]. The margin does not appear: a value search of the body returns zero occurrences of delta and zero of margin, and one occurrence of interpolation, in section 14, inside the sentence that dissolves the tripod worry rather than in any sentence that specifies the mapping. That single occurrence is the Skeptic's one overstatement and it is corrected here rather than passed over, because this arbitration verifies the figures it rules on and that includes the reviewers'.

Taking the Guardian second, his positive half is right in substance and wrong by one step. He is right that five of the six mapping parameters do not read the register triple or the margin at all: dynamics reads trauma, tempo and metre read entropy and trauma, fragmentation and density read the amended A8 pair, and timbre reads the assigned profile, so the triples and the margin block the mode channel and nothing else [9]. He is right that S3 proves the rule is argmax exactly wherever the leader's gap exceeds the margin, with no divergence found on 180,183 enumerated states, so an assignment with a clear leader renders identically for every margin below that leader's gap [9]. He is wrong that this makes three of five gradients buildable and audible now. The table at section 6.3 does not assign a vertex to Dependent, Leader and Resister; it assigns the words Imaginary-weighted, Symbolic-weighted and Real-weighted, and a word has no gap and therefore no margin to be below. The correct statement is one step further back and is better news than the Guardian's, because it identifies a decision rather than a dependency: three of the five triples can be written down now, in five minutes, without settling the margin, and once written the three gradients render and can be heard. The margin is needed for Partner, which is at the barycentre by construction, and for Follower, which sits on an edge and whose position relative to the margin decides whether it collapses onto Dependent. That is one decision and one experiment, not five decisions and a blocked channel.

**The ruling.** The instrument is not specifiable as one object and is specifiable in four parts, in this order, and the document should be restructured to say so rather than to present a single mapping table with five English phrases in it.

The first part is the three parameters that read trauma and entropy alone: dynamics, tempo and metre, and the two texture ladders. These are specifiable today and are blocked by one thing the reviewers treat as a detail and which is in fact the largest single omission on the input side, which is that no gradient has a stated map into a state coordinate. Musical variability to entropy and session intensity to trauma are each one sentence, and until they are written the whole of the engine reads nothing, not merely the mode channel. This is the part of the Skeptic's finding 7 that neither reviewer put first and that the ruling puts first.

The second part is the mode channel at Dependent, Leader and Resister. It is specifiable as soon as three triples are numbers, it does not wait on the margin, and it is the cheapest demonstrable output the programme has.

The third part is the mode channel at Partner and Follower. It waits on the margin, the margin waits on Part B of the listening pack, and Part B exists, is hashed and is one panel sitting away [13]. The document does not record that the number is missing, does not name the experiment, and gates nothing on it, and all three of those are repairs to the document rather than to the world.

The fourth part is the timbre channel, and it is not specifiable in this document at all. It waits on design items 2 and 4 and on the four missing pieces of design section 5b.6, all four of which were reconfirmed here at path and line [5], [17].

**Arousal and valence, and the true statement rather than the comfortable one.** The document spends two of five taps on ratings that drive no music and gates on their anchors. There are two honest resolutions and section 14 already asks the author to choose between them. Either the two are dropped, and the budget falls to three taps, or they are kept for the legibility to the AI music therapy literature that section 5.2 gives as their reason and are marked on the rating surface and in the explanation as recorded and not rendered. The comfortable third option, which is to leave the mapping table silent and let the reader assume a channel exists, is what draft 6 does and is what this ruling refuses. If they are kept, the interaction budget is restated as three taps that drive music and two that are recorded, and the fixture says so, because the alternative is a therapist who changes a rating and hears nothing and concludes the instrument is broken.

**What the ruling costs.** It costs the document its single mapping table and the appearance that the input side is one specification with five holes in it. It costs G1, which as written can be passed with the entire input side unspecified, because none of its five conditions requires a triple, a margin, or a gradient-to-coordinate map; G1 must gain a condition that one passage renders from one rating through a stated map, and that condition currently cannot be met. And it costs the two-of-five admission, which is the most uncomfortable sentence the author will have to write in draft 7 and is true.

## 3. Ruling B. Determinism is claimed and not delivered, and what the document may say today

The Guardian's finding 1 is confirmed at every point and the confirmation is stronger than the finding. `SEED_ALGORITHM_VERSION` exists at `src/lib/deterministic.ts:71` with the value 1, is prefixed into every key at `:121`, and is pinned by a test at `deterministic.test.ts:130`, which is exactly right; a search of `src/components/` and `src/app/` for the identifier returns nothing, so it reaches no export, no score object and no record [17]. No identifier resembling a ruleset version exists anywhere in the tree, and the document nowhere says what a ruleset is. `package.json` declares version 1.0.0 and nothing reads it, while `src/components/mpn-lab/score_exporter.ts:123` writes a hardcoded `version: '2.0.0'` into every exported score, a string that matches no other artefact in the programme and that a reader would take for the build [17]. And there is no seed, because the generator is keyed: `rand` takes a tuple of coordinates, `buildKey` joins them behind the algorithm version and hashes the result, and what reproduces a passage is the coordinate tuple together with the state and the configuration. Recording a seed is not a thing this architecture can be asked to do.

Against that, the Skeptic's finding 17 adds the three things the Guardian's tree inspection cannot see. The S4 measurement the paragraph cites is a measurement of the MPN Conductor, and section 4.3 of this document decides a new codebase, of which two of the three score-path files that make the eleven keyed draws are in the repaired set with their defects named. Determinism on a rebuilt score path with two repaired call sites is a property nobody has established, and section 7.5 asserts it in the present tense. Under revision 9 of S3 the harmonic parameter is not a function on the state space at all, which is repaired only by the author's decision of 14 September, which this document does not cite anywhere [7], [8]. And the configuration the claim quantifies over is incomplete, because the margin and the function f are both unfixed and neither is in the quartet. The User's finding 16 adds the fourth thing, which is that the configuration is named as one of the three arguments to the function and is absent from the record the section specifies, while sections 7.1, 7.2, 7.6 and 10 each add something to it.

**The ruling, stated as what the document may say.**

Section 7.5's requirement stands and is the best reasoning in section 7. The sentence that a version quartet is a promise and a waveform is a record is correct, is S4's own condition applied, and survives unchanged. The sentence that if a therapist cannot reproduce what a client heard last week the record is not a record also stands, and it is the sentence the rest of this ruling is measured against.

Section 7.5's factual sentence does not stand and may not be restated in the present tense. What the document may say is that S4 established zero unseeded draws on the Conductor's score path and eleven keyed draws against eleven key constructions, in the past tense and about the Conductor, and that the rebuild inherits the generator unchanged and owes the same property on a path two of whose call sites are repaired. The forward statement belongs in the gate, which is where it already is, and the gate's outcome may not be stated as a fact in front of it.

The word seed is replaced throughout by the key coordinates the generator consumes. This is not a wording preference. An implementer sent looking for a scalar seed in this architecture will either fail to find one or invent one, and inventing one is worse, because a scalar seed threaded alongside a keyed generator produces two sources of reproducibility that can disagree.

The ruleset version is defined here, because the Guardian is right that a field nobody can populate is not a requirement, and because the definition settles the Skeptic's third point and the User's sixteenth in the same stroke. **The ruleset version numbers everything whose change changes a rendered passage and which is not the code**: the modal and pitch tables, the dynamics ladder, the tempo and metre tables, the two texture ladders, the interpolation margin, the function f inside the harmonic parameter, and the anchor set. That definition puts the margin and f inside the record, which is exactly what the Skeptic's finding requires and what "ruleset version" was never stated to include.

The quartet becomes a provenance block of five parts plus the audio: the key coordinates, the seed algorithm version, the ruleset version as just defined, the application build read from one place, and the configuration, with the configuration itself stated as which parts are per character, per session and per passage. One component writes the block, the exporter carries it, and the record holds it. The hardcoded 2.0.0 in `score_exporter.ts` is a live defect in an artefact section 9.2 already repairs and belongs in that row.

Gate G1's determinism condition is rewritten to gate on the block rather than on "its seed and versions", because as written it names two objects that do not exist and one that cannot.

**What the ruling costs.** It costs the document the strongest present-tense claim it makes, and it costs it in the section where the claim is most rhetorically effective. It costs the author a definition he has not had to write, and the definition is consequential rather than clerical, because putting the margin and f inside the ruleset version means that fixing either is a stimulus-version bump with a migration for any study in flight, which is design section 6.3's own rule reaching back into the Instrument [5]. And it costs section 7.5 the word quartet, which is a good word. The alternative cost is the one the section's own sentence names, which is a record that is not a record, and this ruling takes the first cost to avoid the second.

## 4. Ruling C. The explanation fixture is wrong three times and is withdrawn

Section 7.4 designates its worked explanation "the first fixture in section 8.4's golden-output suite". Three reviewers reach it independently, from the corpus, from buildability and from the therapist's ear, and each finds a different subset of the same three errors. That convergence raises the finding's standing rather than merely duplicating it, and all three are upheld.

The first claim, that Partner sits between leading and following so the music blends the two nearest, is false against every document in the corpus. Section 6.3 puts Partner at or near the barycentre, S3 states that at the barycentre the weights are equal and the result is the mean of the three modes, and design section 11 restates it twice and adds that this is the prediction rather than an artefact, that mutual regulation sounds like the even blend of the three [5], [9]. Three is not two, and the error is not arithmetic: it is the difference between a two-way blend of the Imaginary and the Symbolic and a three-way blend that includes the Real. The User adds the sharper half, which is that the fixture also says no single mode was "clearly indicated", which tells the therapist the instrument was uncertain at the one point the interpolation was built to make well defined. That inverts the epistemic status as well as the arithmetic.

The second claim, that session intensity at 4 of 5 set the volume to the fourth of eight levels, is unreachable from its own input. S3's eight markings are bounded at trauma below 0.10, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50, 0.65, 0.80, 0.90 and 1.00, so the fourth marking requires trauma in the interval from 0.35 to 0.50 [9]. The third claim, that session intensity widened the chord movement by one step, describes the harmonic form the author withdrew on 14 September. Decision D-2026-09-14-B replaces a move of size round of k-max times delta from the chord already sounding with chord position equal to round of 23 times f of the state, around the LR cycle from a fixed origin, expressly to restore decomposability and to remove the first-frame problem [7], [8]. **The ruling.** The fixture is withdrawn as written. It is not corrected in place, for a reason that is the whole of this ruling and that no reviewer states: **a golden fixture cannot be written before the engine it pins is specified**, and three of the four inputs it reads are unspecified today. So the passage is demoted to an illustrative example, marked as one, until the four inputs of ruling A exist; the designation "the first fixture in the golden-output suite" is removed from it; and the suite itself is specified in section 7.4 as a numbered requirement, with the forbidden vocabulary written out as a list the test runs against, which is what discharges S1, G9 and U9 at the same time. The rewritten example, when it can be written, states the even blend of three and says in the same sentence that it is the least distinctive sound the instrument makes, which is the User's finding 6 discharged in the place where the therapist will actually meet it rather than in a second warning on a second surface. It names both inputs to fragmentation. It states the rating-to-trauma map and the marking it produces, or it drops the number. **What the ruling costs.** It costs the document its differentiating output, in the draft, for as long as the input side is unspecified. Section 7.4 is what the 2026 survey reports no reviewed system offering, it is the best argument in the document for building this rather than something else, and this ruling leaves it as a specification with an illustration rather than as a worked output. ## 7. Ruling F. The audible fraction, verified

The brief requires this to be verified before ruling and it was. Under the S3 section 2.6 contrast basis, which is an orthonormal scaled Hadamard matrix whose first row is the null direction, the pair (0.52, 0.52, 0.48, 0.48) against (0.48, 0.48, 0.52, 0.52) has a difference of (0.04, 0.04, -0.04, -0.04), whose magnitude component is exactly zero, whose length is 0.0800 and whose timbre distance is 0.0800, returning an audible fraction of 1.000. The Skeptic's second constructed pair, at a difference of 0.2000, returns 1.000 likewise. The document's own diameter pair returns 1.000 at 2.0000 and the S3 flat pair returns 0.000 at 0.0000. Every figure in the Skeptic's finding 3 reproduces [18].

The ruling is UPHELD-NARROWED, and the narrowing is the substance.

The Skeptic is right that the check detects deafness in one direction only. It is a ratio, it is therefore scale-free, and the failure it cannot see is the much larger one: two profiles too close together to be told apart. A pair at four per cent of the channel's diameter passes the document's check perfectly, and the design's own inversion of the separation curve puts the resolution far above that on every figure either document carries [5]. He is right that a number displayed to a user under the name "audible fraction", returning 1.000 on a pair nobody could hear apart, is the class of defect section 5.1 exists to prohibit, committed in the same draft that restates the rule. And he is right that there is no threshold, which the Guardian's finding 6 and the User's finding 4 reach independently and which design section 7 already lists among the numbers the programme has left unfixed [5].

He is wrong about what the quantity measures. It measures the fraction of the difference between two profiles that survives the projection into the timbre space, and on the pure-shape pair that fraction genuinely is 1.000, because none of the difference lies in the null direction. The quantity is not misnamed in the way `CLINICAL_HEALTH_SCORE` was misnamed, where a row counter was called a measurement of health. It is a correct measurement of the wrong thing, or rather of only one of the two things the requirement needs. The word doing the damage is "audible", which is a perceptual claim that a geometric ratio cannot make, and the fix is not to rename the quantity to something it does not compute but to say what it computes and to add the second number.

**The ruling.** Two numbers, not one. The first is the ratio, renamed to the carried fraction, which is the fraction of the difference between two profiles that the channel carries, with a declared threshold below which the assignment is flagged. The second is the timbre separation itself, in contrast units, checked against the perceptual resolution. A pair fails if either the carried fraction is below its threshold or the separation is below the resolution, and the two failures are different and are reported differently, because the remedy for the first is to change the shape of one profile and the remedy for the second is to move them apart.

The thresholds are unequal in cost and the document should say so. The carried fraction's threshold is a ratio, it waits on no experiment, and it is one decision the author can take at a desk today; it is recorded as a choice in the same way section 11.1 records the weighted-kappa threshold as a choice, so that a later reviewer can disagree with it rather than assume it was derived. The separation threshold is the perceptual resolution and waits on Part D of the listening pack, which is the number design section 5b.5 calls the one that is not ours to invent, and which the Guardian's finding 17 shows is not recoverable from the ladder as built [5], [14]. So the document may require both numbers now, may set one now, and must say that the other is unmeasured and that the experiment built to measure it confounds separation with axis.

**What this costs.** It costs gate G1 a condition it cannot meet, since G1 as written requires the check computed and shown and the check is now two checks of which one has no threshold. G1's timbre condition moves per change 43. And it costs the document the tidiness of a one-line check, which section 7.6 presents as its virtue. A one-line check that is blind to the larger of the two failures it exists to catch is not a virtue, and the second line is the price of the first being true.

## 10. Disposition

**STOP. Thirty-five findings upheld, twenty-one upheld narrowed, six rejected.**

STOP rather than REVISE, which is the Skeptic's disposition against the other two reviewers', and the reason is narrower than the Skeptic's and narrower than the count. Two of the three grounds he gives for STOP do not survive this arbitration. The claim that both of draft 6's additions are keyed to a superseded revision is rejected, because section 7.6 cites revision 8, carries its narrowing, and records TC-1 as reopened. And the count of blocking findings is not itself a ground, because eleven of the Guardian's eleven are closable at a desk and the User's seventeen are largely one omission in seventeen places.

What carries STOP is three things, each of which is a finding against a joint rather than against a sentence.

The first is that the document's central verification mechanism does not exist. Section 5.2 identifies, correctly and better than any other paragraph in the document, that the generated explanation is the one output that cannot be checked by reading the source, and names the golden-output test as what enforces the naming rule on it. That test was deleted with the section that held it, it is specified nowhere in the document, and it exists nowhere in the tree. The one fixture that would have seeded it is wrong in three places and is withdrawn by ruling C. A document whose only enforcement of its only surviving discipline is a pointer into a deleted section is not ready to be built from, and that is the Guardian's own reason for not accepting, arrived at here independently.

The second is that the input side is unspecified in a way that no gate detects. Two of five ratings reach no musical parameter, no gradient has a stated map into a state coordinate, the register triples are words, the margin appears in no sentence, and the function f appears in none either. The instrument can pass G0 and G1 with all of that outstanding, because G0 requires anchors and G1 requires a log, a record, a determinism condition that names two objects that do not exist, and a timbre channel that design section 10a sequences after two items the manifest does not contain. A gate that the thing can pass while producing no music is not a gate on the thing.

The third is question 5a, and it is the one that would carry STOP on its own. Three conditions in this document, including two of the four gates, wait on the return of an experiment that nobody has built and that nothing in the corpus asks. That is not a document defect that a careful pass repairs; it is a fact about the world that the document has been wrong about for two days, and the three conditions have to be rewritten before anything downstream of them can move.

Against all of that, four things in the document should survive every future revision unchanged and are recorded so that a draft 7 does not lose them while repairing the rest. Section 6.4's affirmative-acceptance argument identifies the exact mechanism by which a machine value reaches a user through inaction and closes it, and it identifies the second cost, that an unnoticed default recorded as a decision destroys the log the whole roadmap is built on. Section 6.5's refusal to show a rater their own agreement rate is reasoned from the measurement outward. Section 7.5's statement of what a record has to be is the strongest sentence in section 7 and is the standard ruling B holds the rest of the section to. And section 11.5, which states in advance that none of the four studies closes S1's governing gap and that the programme should stop expecting the next one to, is the kind of paragraph documents of this sort never contain, and both the Guardian and this arbiter would put it first among the things not to touch.

The two-node timbre case is right, and it is right for a reason that no further work can move: the exhaustive enumeration of the sixteen corners is complete, needs no seed, and returns 2.000000, which is the diameter of the reachable set and cannot be exceeded. The document is correct about the one thing it builds its new section on. What ruling F and rulings A and D establish is that it is correct about it while its stated rule for attaining that separation is false, while the check it puts in the build is blind to the larger of the two failures it exists to catch, while three sentences call live a channel that is not, and while the mechanism that would enforce its naming rule was deleted. Repairing those is the work of a draft, not of a research programme, and the shortest path is the four numbers first, then the pointers, then the fixture, then question 5a.
