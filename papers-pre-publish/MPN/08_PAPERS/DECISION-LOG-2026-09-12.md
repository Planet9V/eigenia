## Notes on three of them

**On 1.** Forty-two to twenty-four is a modest reduction and that is the point of it. It also has a tidy internal logic: twenty-four was the size of the wider vector before the bias count was settled at thirty, so reducing forty-two to twenty-four keeps the personality space the size it always was while carrying the whole bias catalogue behind it. S2 must still say plainly that the number is chosen rather than fitted, because no data exists to fit it to.

**On 3.** "Free energy" was doing duty for two different objects: a variational bound on surprise, from the predictive-processing literature, and a potential well in a dynamical model of collapse. Those are not the same quantity and using one name for both is how a reader comes to believe the theory has borrowed a result it has not. The two adopted names say what each thing is and neither invites the other's authority.

**On 5.** This one is not a deferral. Six of eight synthetic raters, at maximum extremity, moved the Symbolic to the semitone above the tonic rather than to a dimmer partner of the reference scale, and that fits none of the four tables in the corpus. If they are right the trauma switch is a jump to a crisis mode per register rather than a dim within a pair, which is a different mechanism from the one A4 states. It is exactly the sort of thing that should be decided by people who score drama, and it is now question 5a in the pack.
