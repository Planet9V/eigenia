Every `[n]` in the paper is listed with what the paper asserts on its authority, quoted from the body rather than from the reference line, and with a status established here rather than carried forward from an earlier ledger. The five statuses are the ones the programme's plan fixes. **VERIFIED** means the source was opened from this session and says what the paper says it says. **VERIFIED-NARROWED** means it was opened, it exists, and it supports part of the claim; the row states which part it does not support. **UNVERIFIED-OFFLINE** means a published source that could not be opened from here; the row says what would be needed. **BROKEN** means the path, line or URL does not resolve; the row gives what is at the location instead. **SELF** means a document in this series; the row names the revision cited and says whether that revision is current.

A word on the boundary between SELF and the other four, because the paper leans on its own series harder than on anything else. A series document whose revision resolves and whose content carries the claim is recorded as SELF, which is a statement about the kind of warrant and not a grade of it. Where a series document was opened and does not carry what the paper attributes to it, the sharper status is given instead and the kind column still says the source is a series document. That keeps a failure inside the series from hiding behind the word SELF, which is the only reason the distinction is worth drawing.

## Claims carried without a citation, on purpose

The four proofs are the paper's own and are cited to nothing, which is correct: Lemma 1, Lemma 2, the scalarisation identity and Theorem 2 are proved in the text and a reader disagrees with the proof rather than with a source. The same applies to the positivity bound of section 5.2 and to the closed forms of section 5.3, each of which is verified by script rather than attributed. Section 6.1's statement that the theory has never defined the codomain of the transformation is a claim about an absence in the corpus, and an absence has no citation; the warrant for it is the source pass recorded in [26].
