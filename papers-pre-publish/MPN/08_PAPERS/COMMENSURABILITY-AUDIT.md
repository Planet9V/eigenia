## 1. Method, and the one thing the arithmetic says

A weight multiplies a variable; the influence it buys is the weight times that variable's dispersion. For a combination with terms $w_k X_k$, the effective contribution of term $k$ is

$$\frac{|w_k|\,\sigma_k}{\sum_j |w_j|\,\sigma_j}.$$

Two consequences govern every row below. First, for any two-input combination the nominal and effective weights agree if and only if the two inputs have equal dispersion, so the agreement condition is always $k = \sigma_X/\sigma_Y = 1$ and the interesting number is the absolute dispersion the unknown input would have to carry. Where one input's dispersion is known from the frame file, that absolute value is stated. Second, an input with zero dispersion on the path where the formula runs contributes nothing whatever its weight, and the formula collapses to the other inputs plus a constant. That second case is the expensive one and it is not rare.

Verdicts are AGREES, MISLEADING and DEGENERATE as instructed. A fourth label, UNDETERMINED, is used where at least one input's dispersion is not measurable from any data in the programme. Assigning MISLEADING to those would be inventing the finding, and assigning AGREES would be worse. UNDETERMINED is not a pass: it records that the weight is an undeclared claim about a dispersion nobody has measured, which is exactly the condition Recommendation 1 exists to end.

Dispersions are population standard deviations, matching the convention of `05_DATA/03_generators/a8_form_analysis.py:351` and of MPN-NOTE-01 section 2. No dispersion, coefficient or line number below is estimated; every figure is either read from the source or computed from the named file.

## 4. Quantities with more than one incompatible definition

Six named quantities carry two or more definitions that are not reconcilable. Where a weighted combination is one of the competing definitions, the audit row is given.

**Tension has five definitions.** As `0.9·real + 0.1·entropy` at `src/components/mpn-lab/psychometric_calculus.ts:310` (rows 4 and 15). As `0.4τ + 0.3H + 0.3(1 − BSI)` at `ml/psychoscore_v2/models/mckenney_lacan_calculus.py:281-287` (row 14). As $T = 0.4 x_p + 0.3 s + 0.3 \ell$ at `08_PAPERS/MPN-2-notation.md:240` (row 16), which section 4.1 of that paper explicitly declares to be the only tension index. As a lookup on a chord symbol at `psychometric_calculus.ts:220-230`, where `Tritone` is 0.9 and `C Major` is 0.0. And as trauma itself, in the conductor page's fallback `harmony: { chord: script.chord, tension: trauma }` at `src/app/mpn-conductor/page.tsx:505`. The first three are weighted combinations over three disjoint input sets: a register and entropy, trauma and entropy and a register balance, and three operational indices. They cannot all be the tension index.

## 6. What the next paper must not state without qualification

In priority order, by how far the stated coefficient is from what the quantity actually does.

First, the composite stability score. No paper may print "BSI 60 per cent, dynamic stability 40 per cent" as `12_LYAPUNOV_STABILITY.md:164-167` does.
