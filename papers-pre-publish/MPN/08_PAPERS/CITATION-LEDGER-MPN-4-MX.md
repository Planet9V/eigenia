# Citation ledger: MPN-4-MX-R (closed research environment edition of the deployment paper)

Compiled 2026-09-12 for `MPN-4-MX-deployment-mexico.md` in its research-edition rewrite. Every European legal source is likewise dropped: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and its amending Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the Commission guidelines C(2025) 5052 and C(2025) 5053, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Wet op de ondernemingsraden, the Article 29 Working Party opinion on data processing at work, the EU-Mexico Global Agreement and the USMCA.

## Items deliberately not cited or not quoted

- ISO 7731 and IEC 62443-2-1: both belonged to the field deployment (danger-signal audibility at a live console, a site security programme) and have no referent in a closed synthetic environment; neither is cited.
- The SAFTE-FAST field-validation and regulator-guidance sources (Roma et al. 2012; CASA guidance; FRA final rule): the fatigue channel survives as a generated channel under DM-8, and no advisory-model precedent is relied on, so none is cited.
- NUREG-0711: observer-rated crew evaluation is not used as a precedent in this edition; not cited.
- Speech-stress systematic reviews: existence verified in the series research but unreadable; the corpus-specificity of reported speech accuracies is attributed in the paper to the working group's own reading, not to a cited review.
- No coefficient, page number, lead time or accuracy figure is quoted anywhere in the paper; the SD4 reliabilities, the HRV standard measures and the anaesthesia latencies are cited by source without numbers except the one figure carried from Loeb and Fitch (sixty per cent identification from sound alone), which the series ledger records as VERIFIED.
