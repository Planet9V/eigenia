## 5. Test 4: what the unfixed triples and delta block, and what they do not

Stated compactly before the findings, because the useful half of the answer is the second half.

**Blocked.** The mode channel entirely. The interpolation weights are w_k = max(0, 1 - (m - x_k)/delta) normalised, so without the five triples there is no x to read and without delta there is no divisor; the channel has neither an input nor a scale. Since every one of the five gradients renders through that channel, no passage renders at all. With it go the collapse check of design revision 8 section 11, which is defined as an enumeration of the five triples against the chosen margin and the live table, and the question of whether Partner is distinguishable from Dependent and Follower, which is what the collapse check exists to answer. The section 7.4 explanation fixture is blocked too, since it asserts what Partner sounds like.

**Not blocked, and this is most of the instrument.** Five of the six Phi parameters do not read the register triple or the margin. Dynamics is a function of session intensity under S3 section 2.1, tempo and metre of entropy and trauma under 2.2, fragmentation and orchestration density of the amended A8 pair under 2.4, and timbre of the assigned DISC profile under 2.6. The harmonic parameter is now unblocked on its constant, k_max having been fixed at 23 on 14 September, and is blocked instead on f, which is finding 4. Nothing about the rating surface, the anchors, the agreement log, the RG-MBI record, the four determinism fields, the stop control, the nothing-plays-unbidden rule, the S3-1 and S3-2 repairs, or the VexFlow and MIDI and audio paths reads either quantity. Neither does the timbre channel, whose blockers are entirely separate.

One more thing is not blocked and is worth stating because it is free. S3 section 2.3 proves that wherever the gap exceeds delta the rule is argmax exactly, with no divergence found on 180,183 enumerated states. So any gradient assigned at or near a vertex renders identically for every positive delta. Dependent, Leader and Resister are vertex-weighted by section 6.3's own table, so three of the five gradients can be built, heard and demonstrated before delta is fixed at all. Only Partner, which is at the barycentre by construction, and Follower, which sits on the Imaginary to Symbolic edge and may or may not fall inside the margin, actually wait on the number.

## 8. Findings

### Finding 1. The build emits none of the four fields the record needs, and one of them is not a thing that exists (b). BLOCKING.

Section 7.5 requires that the record carry the seed, the `SEED_ALGORITHM_VERSION`, the ruleset version and the application build, and G1 gates on "determinism reproducing a passage from its seed and versions". Taking the four in order against the tree, the first does not exist as a concept. The generator is keyed, not seeded: `src/lib/deterministic.ts` builds a key from `SEED_ALGORITHM_VERSION` and a tuple of coordinates at line 121 and hashes it, so what reproduces a passage is the coordinate tuple that `characterKey` and `frameKey` construct, together with the state and the configuration, and there is no scalar seed anywhere to record. Recording a seed is therefore not a thing the build can be asked to do, and the requirement as phrased sends an implementer looking for an object the architecture does not contain. The second exists at `deterministic.ts:71` with the value 1 and is pinned by `src/lib/__tests__/deterministic.test.ts:130`, which is exactly right, but a search of `src/components/` and `src/app/` for `SEED_ALGORITHM_VERSION` returns nothing, so it reaches no export, no score object and no record. The third does not exist: no identifier resembling a ruleset version appears in the tree, and the PRD nowhere says what a ruleset is, whether it is the Phi table set, the bias layer, the anchor set or all three, so the field cannot be populated even by a willing implementer. The fourth does not exist either: `package.json` declares `"version": "1.0.0"` and nothing reads it, while `src/components/mpn-lab/score_exporter.ts:123` writes a hardcoded `version: '2.0.0'` into every exported score, a string that matches neither the package version nor any other artefact and that an unwary reader would take for the build. The repair is small and has three parts: replace "the seed" with the key coordinates the generator actually consumes, define what a ruleset version numbers, and thread all of it plus the package version into one provenance block that the exporter writes and the record carries. Until then G1's determinism condition is not a question, and section 7.5's own sentence that a therapist who cannot reproduce what a client heard last week has no record stands against the build rather than for it.

### Finding 2. Delta is unfixed and the mode channel has no divisor (b). BLOCKING.

S3 section 2.3 defines the interpolation weights with delta in the denominator and says in terms that the margin "is the author's to fix". The PRD does not fix it, does not mention it, and does not name it as an open question in section 14, so a reader of the PRD alone would not know that the mode channel has an unset parameter in it. `MPN-NOTE-05` section 1 records that delta is no longer one of the blocking numbers that must be settled by argument, because the listening pack now carries the experiment, which is true and is confirmed at test 4 below: Part B of the pack puts the near-tie pair at two margins to listeners and the facilitator note states the reading, that if listeners separate them at 17.4 cents the smaller margin is doing audible work and delta can be small, and if they do not, the larger margin is the honest choice. So the experiment exists, the stimuli exist and are hashed, and the number is one panel away. What is blocking is not the difficulty of getting it; it is that the PRD does not record that the number is missing, does not name the experiment that would fix it, and gates nothing on it, while G1 requires a rendered passage that cannot be produced without it. Section 14 should carry delta as an open question with the pack named beside it, and section 5.4's mapping row for the register triple should say that the mapping is parameterised by a margin that is not yet set.

### Finding 3. The five Autonomy register triples are words rather than numbers (b). BLOCKING.

Section 6.3's table gives the five gradients as Imaginary-weighted, Imaginary to Symbolic, balanced near the barycentre, Symbolic-weighted and Real-weighted. Under interpolation the exact coordinates are the blend weights, so near the barycentre and at the barycentre are different music and the table cannot be read by a renderer at all. Design revision 8 section 11 makes this the first of its four conditions and says the same thing in the same terms, five triples as numbers in the code beside the two warnings the PRD already requires. The PRD records the condition at section 14 and does not act on it, and no other document supplies the numbers. Three of the five are cheap, since Dependent, Leader and Resister are vertex-weighted and any assignment at or near a vertex renders identically for every positive delta, per the argmax agreement S3 proves on 180,183 states. Partner is fixed by construction at (1/3, 1/3, 1/3). Follower is the only genuinely open one, because it sits on an edge and its position relative to the margin decides whether it collapses onto Dependent. So the work here is one decision rather than five, and until it is taken nothing renders.

### Finding 4. The harmonic parameter is round(23 f(state)) and f is unnamed (b). BLOCKING.

The author's decision of 14 September fixed k_max at 23 and adopted the absolute form, which restores decomposability for the one parameter of six that lacked it and removes the first-frame problem. `MPN-S3-AMENDMENTS-2` section 3 records what the decision did not do, under the heading "One loose end, which is the author's": the decision fixes the form and the constant and leaves f unnamed, and the amendment declines to adopt the one live candidate, the tension 0.9r + 0.1H, for three stated reasons. It is a function of the Real and entropy rather than of trauma, so adopting it would change what the parameter is a function of. At the library's dispersions its nominal ninety to ten is effectively 94.9 to 5.1, so it is very nearly the Real alone. And on the 164 frames of 232 that carry no Real keyword the Real is exactly zero, so tension reduces to 0.1H in [0, 0.1], which under round(23 f) confines the chord to three of twenty-four triads on roughly seven frames in ten. The PRD never mentions f, k_max or the harmonic parameter's form at all, yet section 7.3's RG-MBI row 3b requires harmonic material to be captured automatically on every record, and every rendered passage sounds a chord. The PRD should carry f as an open question in section 14 with the amendment's three warnings attached, so that whoever answers it knows that the obvious answer collapses the channel.

### Finding 5. The gate condition "question 5a returned" cannot be satisfied, because the listening pack does not ask it (b). BLOCKING.

Question 5a is defined by decision 5 of the log of 12 September and by S3 section 2.3 as a question about A4's second stage: whether the trauma switch at tau = 0.6 is a dim within a pair, as the shipped table has it, or a jump to a crisis mode per register, as six of eight synthetic raters read it, which "fits none of the four tables in the corpus" and would change A4's mechanism rather than its assignment. Three places in the PRD gate on its return. Section 9.2 bars any modal table repair until it returns. Section 11.2 makes it an entry condition on Study 2. G2 and G3 both name it. The pack that exists is version 2, built 14 September, and its own claims table names the four claims it tests: Part A, that three kinds of dramatic predicament each belong with a particular scale; Part B, that blending two scales at a tie produces a usable musical object; Part C, that the formal distance between two triads is audible as a magnitude; Part D, that three timbre dimensions can carry six distinguishable characters. The facilitator note repeats the same four and adds that Part A settles "A4's register-to-mode table, which four documents disagree about". **Not one of the four is A4's second stage.** The string "5a" does not appear in the pack at all. Part A holds seven scales on one melody and asks respondents to rank six of them and to match four situations, which is an instrument for choosing among the corpus's four competing tables, and a table is precisely what decision 5 says 5a is not about. So the bar of section 9.2 can never lift, Study 2 can never start, and G2 and G3 can never clear, not because the answer is hard but because nothing in the corpus asks the question. Either a fifth part is added to the pack, putting the dim and the jump readings of the second stage to the same panel, or the three gate conditions are rewritten to name Part A and to say plainly that they now wait on the table rather than on the mechanism.

### Finding 6. The audible-fraction check has no threshold (b). BLOCKING.

Section 7.6 requires the instrument to compute the audible fraction of every assigned pair and show it, and says that "an assignment that comes out near zero is declared on the therapist's screen" and that "if the ratio is near one the two are inaudible". G1 gates on the check being "computed and shown". The computation is exact and needs nothing: because the contrast basis is orthonormal the map is an orthogonal projection followed by an isometry, so the fraction is one minus the squared magnitude component of the difference over its squared length, and design revision 8 section 5b.2 works three examples, two at zero and one at 0.240. What has no value anywhere is the number at which the instrument declares. Design revision 8 section 7 says so in its own list of unfixed numeric parameters, naming "the audible-fraction threshold of section 5b, which PRD section 7.6 leaves as near zero with no value in either document". A requirement whose trigger is the phrase "near zero" cannot be implemented, cannot be tested, and cannot be gated on, and the failure it exists to prevent, two clinically different characters rendered with the same timbre, is exactly the kind that is invisible without a declared trigger. The number is cheap and does not wait on any experiment, because the fraction is a ratio rather than a distance: a value of 0.1 or 0.2 declared in the PRD would discharge the requirement today.

### Finding 7. Section 7.6 rests on a design item that revision 8 withdraws (c). BLOCKING.

Section 7.6 states: "Item TC-1 of the design, six families keyed to the six canonical contrast directions, fixes both and is small [17]." That was D51 of revision 7. Revision 8 section 5b.4 withdraws it in terms: "A label that is a deterministic function of the three coordinates it labels carries no information those coordinates do not already carry, and S3 section 2.6 put the family parameter there precisely to hold the categorical specificities that lie on no shared dimension. The six-direction key solves the tie by deleting the channel. The diagnosis stands and the remedy is withdrawn: TC-1 is restated as an open problem and it is the author's, being a question about what the family parameter is for." Three things follow for the PRD. The remedy it calls small is not available. The problem it calls fixed is open and is the author's rather than an implementer's. And the problem is worse than section 7.6 states, because converging the search made every profile in the optimal set sit within 0.05 of a tie rather than some of them, which the verification file records at E.7a as three distinct families of six at a six-character cast with all six near a tie. Section 7.6's own sentence, that the maximally separated pair sits exactly on a tie so the family label is undefined precisely where the continuous channel is best, is correct and is now the whole of the finding rather than half of it. The paragraph should be rewritten to say that the family channel is an open problem the author holds, and G1 should not gate on a timbre channel whose categorical half has no specification.

### Finding 8. "Buildable the whole time" is the one sentence revision 8 exists to withdraw (c). BLOCKING.

Section 7.6 closes: "What it gives is a channel that was written off across two documents and has been buildable the whole time." Revision 8 section 5b.6 opens: "Revision 7 said the timbre channel is live because the profiles are ours to choose, and scheduled it as work depending on nothing. Both halves were checked against the reference implementation at path and line, and both are wrong." It then names four missing pieces and says they are missing in sequence, so supplying any one alone changes nothing: the contrast map does not exist in the code in any form, there is no input path for a profile, the family selector is an argmax over the four coordinates, and the default puts every unnamed character at the centre of the reachable set. All four were checked against the tree for this review and all four hold, with the corrections at finding 16. Design section 10a sequences the channel as item 5a, "After 2 and after 4", depending on item 2's rating store and item 4's audio path, and states that "Revision 7's claim that the six canonical profiles need no further work is deleted". G1 requires "DISC assigned per character so the timbre channel renders", so G1 as written depends on two design items that are not in the PRD's own carry-over set and on a map that nothing in the programme has implemented. The section should say what the channel costs rather than that it costs nothing, and G1 should either inherit the design's sequencing or drop the timbre condition to G2.

### Finding 9. The naming rule's only enforcement mechanism points at a section that no longer exists, and seven further references dangle with it (c). BLOCKING.

Section 5.2 states the naming rule, says it binds every surface a client or a non-specialist reader can reach including the generated explanation, and then names its enforcement: "Section 8.4's golden-output test is what enforces this on the explanation, which is generated and therefore cannot be checked by reading the source." The reasoning is exactly right and the mechanism does not exist. Draft 6 replaced section 8 with nine hundred words of prose carrying no subsections and no test, so 8.4 is not a section and the golden-output suite is specified nowhere in the document. It is not in the tree either: a search of `src/` and `tests/` for a golden suite returns only `deterministic.test.ts`, which pins RNG values and not rendered text. So the document's most carefully argued verification requirement, for the one output that cannot be checked by reading source, has no mechanism at all. Seven further references dangle the same way, and they are listed here because they share one cause and one repair. ### Finding 10. The flagship explanation fixture contradicts the mapping it explains (c). BLOCKING.

### Finding 11. Section 14's third condition can never be discharged, because the rule it waits for has been abolished (c). BLOCKING.

Section 14 records four conditions attaching to the Autonomy ruling and gives the third as "keep Partner away from a diatonic renderer until the rounding rule exists". That was revision 7's condition and it is superseded. On 14 September the author took decision B, that Phi does not round: it emits the blended scale degree as a continuous value in cents and the notated pitch is its rounded pre-image, a property of the notation renderer rather than of the mapping. `MPN-S3-AMENDMENTS-2` section 2 states the consequence plainly, that "S4 owes no rounding rule, because there is none inside Phi to owe". Design revision 8 section 11 draws the conclusion for this very condition: "Under D58 there is no rounding rule inside Phi to wait for. What replaces the condition is a disclosure, in three parts." So the PRD instructs the build to hold Partner away from the notation path until an object comes into existence that a decision has just abolished, which is a condition that cannot be discharged, cannot be evaluated and cannot be waived by anybody reading the PRD alone. Worse, the three-part disclosure that replaces it is absent from the PRD entirely, and all three parts are requirements on a rendered output a therapist sees. The renderer must declare on the face of the score which rounding convention it used, and the choice is consequential rather than cosmetic: at an exact tie every degree on which the two modes differ lands on a half, and on the live table's Dorian against Lydian tie, rounding half up returns Lydian exactly, one of the two inputs, while rounding half to even returns a scale that is none of the seven and is what a Python implementation gets by writing `round`. The renderer must also print, beside the score, that notation and audio disagree by up to a quarter tone inside the margin, on the share of the simplex S3's table gives, 9.8 per cent at delta = 0.05 and 36.0 per cent at delta = 0.20. Section 14's third condition should be replaced by the disclosure, and the disclosure belongs in section 7.1 with the other outputs rather than in an open-questions list.

## 10. Disposition

**REVISE. Eleven blocking findings.**

Not REJECT. Nothing found here makes the instrument unbuildable for the uses the author has named. Every blocking finding is a number that has not been chosen, a reference that points at a section which has been deleted, or a claim inherited from a design revision that has since been superseded, and all eleven are closable without new research. Four of them, the delta margin, the five triples, the audible-fraction threshold and the function f, are single decisions the author can take at a desk. Four more, the dangling section references, the fixture's two-mode sentence, the superseded rounding condition and the withdrawn TC-1 remedy, are edits to this document that follow from rulings already made. Two, the determinism quartet and the timbre channel's sequencing, are specification work of a morning. One, question 5a, needs a fifth part added to a pack whose stimuli, generator and facilitator notes already exist.

Not ACCEPT. Five of the eleven make a gate unevaluable or a requirement unimplementable as written, and one of those, the golden-output test, is the only stated enforcement of the naming rule on the one output surface that cannot be checked by reading source. A document whose central verification mechanism cites a section it deleted is not ready to be built from.

The shortest path to a draft 7 that clears is the four decisions first, since three gates and most of section 7 unblock behind them, then the reference update from design revision 7 to revision 8 with `MPN-S3-AMENDMENTS-2` added beside S3, then the eight dangling references, then question 5a.
