Modern industrial infrastructure operates across complex, risk-indexed regulatory environments, where compliance complexity in each jurisdiction dictates the scope of sovereign risk management. Fragmented standards and disconnected supply chain data prevent authoritative tracking of asset composition and vulnerabilities across international shipping corridors.

Supported by a formal research and development grant from the Dutch government for our Cyber Digital Twin platform, we propose this comprehensive cyber digital twin pilot project for a ground station, a Gen 1 satellite, and a Gen 2 satellite across a three-month timeline. 

Our platform ingests engineering documents, product specification documents, interface control documents, system design documents, bills of materials (software, hardware, cryptography, machine, operations), concepts of operations, hazard logs, IEC 62343 analysis, safety programs, cell reliability analyses, minimum operating requirements, and others—converting them into a unified schema mapping all physical, electrical, and computational boundaries with full data provenance.

The platform implements compliance checks such as the Cyber Resilience Act across all assets, such as satellites and site hardware, and integrates bills of materials and vulnerability data for automatic vulnerability and exploitability correlation and prioritization.

Our Cyber Digital Twin platform also executes consequence-driven threat propagation simulations, generates design recommendations, and tracks performance against success metrics including audit time reduction, remediation cycle time, engineering redesign costs, hardware compliance, and lifecycle asset configuration precision.

The pilot project uses a hierarchical model where individual facility twins are integrated into a broader corporate and supply chain ecosystem, scaling securely to include a supply corridor jurisdictional analysis that addresses regulatory friction between component origin and deployment location.

For the pilot, we propose to install and operate our cyber digital twin platform on a secure, dedicated instance within Amazon's cloud infrastructure, ensuring security of the data and inference models.

Total investment for this three-month engagement ranges from 270,000 to 280,000 US dollars. Readout workshops validate deliverables, define specific roles, establish communication cadence, and finalize sign-off criteria.
