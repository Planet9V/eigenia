OXOT Cyber Digital Twin

Product Sheet

Industrial infrastructure operates across risk-indexed regulatory environments, where compliance complexity in each jurisdiction dictates the scope of sovereign risk management. Fragmented standards and disconnected supply chain data prevent authoritative tracking of asset composition and vulnerabilities across international shipping corridors.

OXOT models what you operate from your own engineering diagrams, binds live cyber risk to every asset, records where each component came from and where it is deployed, and rolls the result into one risk-indexed view: consequence weighted by the regulatory environment the asset actually sits in.

1 · Model the asset, in its jurisdiction

  • Builds an as-is model and asset register from your own engineering diagrams
  • Integrates safety, reliability and minimum operating requirements, not only IT data
  • Standardised on DEXPI 2.0 for engineering topology and CycloneDX for the bill of materials, extended across software, hardware, cryptography, manufacturing, operations and cloud services
  • IEC 62443 and TS 50701 carried in the model, so security levels are derived from consequence rather than assigned by asset class
  • Jurisdiction is a property of the asset, not a spreadsheet beside it

2 · Index the risk against the environment it sits in

  • Component origin and deployment destination held in the model, so the obligations attaching to each part are visible alongside the part
  • Obligation transfer made explicit: whoever places the finished asset on a market inherits what is inside it
  • Evidence sufficiency checked per destination market, because documentation adequate in one regime is frequently insufficient in another
  • Corridor view across the path from manufacture through integration to deployment
  • Rollup at every altitude: component → equipment → line → site → jurisdiction → enterprise

3 · Monitor the outside, continuously

  • WorldMonitor: geopolitical unrest, economic uncertainty, political instability and military action, watched per site
  • Value-chain watch: upstream and downstream partners assessed continuously
  • Supplier risk-rating: software and product suppliers scored on track record, to inform buying
  • Threat monitoring: ATQ threat-actor profiling and TACAM consequence-based prioritisation

4 · Simulate and prioritise

  • Maps network pathways to your critical-items list and critical systems, with their consequences
  • Monte Carlo simulation against the model, continuously, with permutations
  • Separates vulnerabilities from exploitable vulnerabilities by reachable pathway, not severity score alone
  • Ranks NOW / NEXT / NEVER, with written permission to ignore the rest
  • Attaches ALE, OXOT’s own model of annualised financial exposure

5 · Decide from one number, drill to the evidence

  • The OXOT Consequence Index: one risk figure for the organisation, rolled up from equipment to enterprise and weighted by jurisdiction
  • World map, site and asset-register views: risk at every altitude
  • Board and engineers reading the same model rather than two reports
  • Conformity evidence from the same model: the CRA Annex VII technical file, IEC 62443, TS 50701 and NIS2

6 · Deploy in the jurisdiction that governs you

  • Single-tenant: your own instance, never pooled
  • AWS European Sovereign Cloud for EU data residency, another region where your obligations require it, or your own premises
  • Passive-first: no agents on your controllers
  • Delivered as a long-term licence, or as a 60-day assisted CRA Transit engagement for one self-assessed product

The Consequence Index, ALE and simulations are OXOT’s own transparent, drillable calculations, directionally validated on real engagements. They are not rating-agency or actuarial marks. Sample figures used in demonstrations are illustrative and are not customer data.
