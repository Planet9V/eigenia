## 📍 Post 1 of 3: Beyond CAD — The Cyber-Physical Digital Twin

Traditional digital twins focus on 3D geometry or basic mechanical wear. But in modern industrial control systems, water networks, and energy grids, physical assets don't operate in isolation—they depend on continuous network telemetry, control loops, and software logic.

A digital twin that models physical parameters while ignoring control network dynamics is incomplete.

At **Eigenia B.V.**, we build **Cyber-Physical Digital Twins** that combine:
1. **Deterministic Physical Laws:** Modeling thermal runaway thresholds, vibration harmonics, flow rates, and rate-of-change frequency drift.
2. **Control & Network Topology:** Mapping sensor protocols, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), and network gateways.
3. **Graph Gated Neural Networks (GGNNs):** Calculating non-linear risk propagation across interconnected infrastructure nodes.

By linking physical constraints directly to control system topology, our digital twin engine allows operators to simulate system behavior accurately under extreme stress.

**Our mission:** Securing clean water, healthy food, and sustainable energy for our (grand)children through complex systems science.

## 📍 Post 2 of 3: Modeling Non-Linear Failure with Graph Gated Neural Networks (GGNNs)

When a critical pump fails in a water treatment plant or a sub-station trips on an energy grid, the failure rarely stays local. It cascades across physical and digital boundaries.

Traditional linear models fail to capture these multi-node cascades.

At **Eigenia B.V.**, we use **Graph Gated Neural Networks (GGNNs)** to power our cyber-physical digital twins.

Here is how it works:
- **Node-Level Physics:** Every physical asset (compressor, valve, transformer, controller) is modeled as a node with specific physical boundaries and operating tolerances.
- **Gated Message Passing:** As telemetry flows through the network, GGNNs pass state updates between adjacent nodes, capturing non-linear interactions.
- **Risk Traversal:** The model calculates how a perturbation—whether an unexpected load spike or a control signal delay—propagates through the physical topology.

By mapping industrial control systems into gated graph structures, we reveal systemic vulnerabilities before they lead to real-world outages.

## 📍 Post 3 of 3: Calculating the Blast Radius — Safe Stress-Testing for Critical Infrastructure

How do you stress-test an industrial plant, a clean water facility, or a power grid without risking physical damage or operational downtime?

You don't test on the live plant. You run the scenario through a Cyber-Physical Digital Twin.

At **Eigenia B.V.**, our simulation engine calculates the **operational blast radius** of complex system failure scenarios.

By running "what-if" simulations inside the twin, we can answer critical questions:
- What happens to water distribution if a pumping station loses power during a peak load window?
- How does a communication lag in an energy storage controller affect grid frequency stability?
- Which secondary nodes absorb system stress, and which ones break?

Our goal is simple: finding the irreducible truth of complex processes so critical infrastructure stays safe, resilient, and reliable.
