## 1. Executive Summary & Domain Scope
DEXPI (Data Exchange in the Process Industry) establishes a vendor-neutral standard for process engineering diagrams, specifically Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs), Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs), and Block Flow Diagrams (BFDs). DEXPI 2.0 (released October 10, 2025) unified previous disjointed specifications into a single DEXPI XML serialization backed by the ISO 15926 Reference Data Library (RDL).

In August 2026, DEXPI e.V. announced the **DEXPI Profile**, an explicit extension mechanism designed to allow domain-specific constraints and attributes on existing classes without forking the base specification.

## 2. Verbatim Key Passages & Data Points

### On Identity & Scope of TagName:
> "In DEXPI/ISO 15926-based P&ID models, every plant item (pump, vessel, pipeline, instrument) is a tagged object with a TagName unique only within the scope of a plant; the plant itself is unique within a site."
> *(Source: DEXPI P&ID Specification 1.4 / 2.0, equipment definition)*

> "ISO 15926-14 explicitly models P&ID equipment as `FunctionalObject` instances whose tags represent design-level equipment; the manufactured `PhysicalObject` is later installed at that functional location."
> *(Source: READI JIP ISO 15926-14 Deliverable, Section 4.2)*

### On Nozzles and Topological Ports:
> "Components like nozzles have sub-tag identities that are unique only within their parent tag (e.g., Nozzle N1 on Vessel V-101). Nozzles represent the physical connection points for piping segments."
> *(Source: DEXPI Equipment & Nozzle Specification)*

### On Extension Mechanism (DEXPI Profile & GenericAttributes):
> "The DEXPI Specification Teams are developing the DEXPI Profile, which extends the DEXPI specification with a mechanism for defining explicit constraints on classes and properties, creating the basis for the DEXPI Process Type Library."
> *(Source: DEXPI e.V. August 2026 Update)*

> "GenericAttributes provide a standardized mechanism within the DEXPI plant model to attach arbitrary key-value sets governed by URIs to any tagged equipment or pipeline object without modifying the core XML schema."
> *(Source: DEXPI 2.0 Schema Documentation)*

## 3. Evaluative Analysis for G_CPDT
- **Identity limitation**: `TagName` (e.g., `P-101`) cannot serve as a global join key because it collides across facilities and does not track physical asset replacement.
- **Topological value**: Contains exact physical piping connections, nozzle dimensions, fluid types, design pressures, and operating temperatures needed for thermodynamic blast radius calculation.
- **Extension compliance**: The 4-attribute join set (`AssetReference`, `AssetReferenceRelation`, `AssetReferenceAuthority`, `AssetReferenceBasis`) can be attached non-destructively via `GenericAttributes Set="AssetJoin"` or as a registered DEXPI Profile.
